📚 Study

Grammar & discourse

  • Present Simple in natural contexts
  • Past Simple when relevant
  • Cause: because
  • Contrast: but, although, whereas, however
  • Consequence: so, as a result

Vocabulary & expressions

  • head out, set off, catch up on
  • get down to, knock off
  • rush hour, hit the road
  • bite the bullet, call it a day
  • Evening and unwinding expressions

What will you learn?

You will develop more natural and flexible English for describing your own routine and another person’s routine in real contexts.

  • Natural Present Simple in context
  • Richer routine and commute vocabulary
  • Phrasal verbs and idiomatic expressions
  • Cause, contrast and consequence
  • More developed speaking and writing

How will we work?

Realistic texts, listening, language noticing and progressive production.

  • Listen to authentic-style routines
  • Notice useful chunks
  • Practise shadowing and pronunciation
  • Build richer spoken and written answers
  • Reflect and improve your final version

Curriculum competences

This Learning Situation develops the Catalan curriculum for Foreign Language through a more autonomous, comparative and reflective challenge.

  • CE3 · Oral communication: describe, compare and explain routines using a clear, coherent spoken text.
  • CE5 · Written & multimodal production: create and revise a coherent Google Slides presentation for a real audience.
  • CPSAA · Personal, social & learning to learn: reflect critically on routines, balance and wellbeing.
  • Digital competence: plan, create and communicate a multimodal product responsibly.

How the challenge is assessed

The final product is connected directly to these assessment criteria:

  • 3.1: produce a structured, coherent oral text to describe, compare, explain and justify ideas.
  • 3.2: plan, produce and review the presentation using appropriate strategies and digital resources.
  • 3.3: interact and collaborate actively and respectfully with a classmate.
  • 5.1: write and publish a coherent, accurate multimodal text of appropriate length in Google Slides.
  • 5.2: plan, produce and revise the written product with increasing autonomy.

🎬 Final product

Describe, compare and reflect

Use a real classmate interview to prepare Google Slides and a camera-and-slides video. Describe the classmate in every route, add comparison in Safe and Daring, and justify a health reflection at your route’s level.

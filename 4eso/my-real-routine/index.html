My Real Routine

En Insecure trobaràs instruccions i ajuda en català: observa els models i practica pas a pas. L’anglès és la llengua dels textos i de la teva producció. Safe redueix l’ajuda i Daring demana més autonomia. Segueix el Learning Path en ordre i prepara les diapositives i el vídeo final amb la guia de Final Product.

Use richer, more natural English to describe everyday life, habits, commuting, responsibilities and the way people unwind.

Your challenge

Compare real routines and reflect on habits

Interview a classmate, prepare Google Slides and record your face and slides together. All routes reflect on health; Safe and Daring compare both routines.

What will you learn?

You will develop more natural and flexible English for describing your own routine and another person’s routine in real contexts.

  • Natural Present Simple in context
  • Richer routine and commute vocabulary
  • Phrasal verbs and idiomatic expressions
  • Cause, contrast and consequence
  • More developed speaking and writing

How will we work?

Realistic texts, listening, language noticing and progressive production.

  • Listen to authentic-style routines
  • Notice useful chunks
  • Practise shadowing and pronunciation
  • Build richer spoken and written answers
  • Reflect and improve your final version

Curriculum competences

This Learning Situation develops the Catalan curriculum for Foreign Language through a more autonomous, comparative and reflective challenge.

  • CE3 · Oral communication: describe, compare and explain routines using a clear, coherent spoken text.
  • CE5 · Written & multimodal production: create and revise a coherent Google Slides presentation for a real audience.
  • CPSAA · Personal, social & learning to learn: reflect critically on routines, balance and wellbeing.
  • Digital competence: plan, create and communicate a multimodal product responsibly.

How the challenge is assessed

The final product is connected directly to these assessment criteria:

  • 3.1: produce a structured, coherent oral text to describe, compare, explain and justify ideas.
  • 3.2: plan, produce and review the presentation using appropriate strategies and digital resources.
  • 3.3: interact and collaborate actively and respectfully with a classmate.
  • 5.1: write and publish a coherent, accurate multimodal text of appropriate length in Google Slides.
  • 5.2: plan, produce and revise the written product with increasing autonomy.

Insecure

La rutina del meu company o companya

Entrevista un company o companya i descriu només la seva rutina. Pren notes, prepara Google Slides i grava la teva cara amb les diapositives. Inclou una reflexió guiada sobre si els seus hàbits són saludables; no comparis amb la teva rutina.

  • ordena el dia amb frases curtes i ajuda en català
  • he / she + Present Simple i expressions treballades
  • valoració senzilla: I think his/her routine is… because… amb un detall de l’entrevista
  • al vídeo es veuen la cara i les diapositives i se sent la veu

Entrevista → Notes → Google Slides → Prepara → Grava la presentació → Revisa → Lliura. Grava les diapositives amb la teva cara visible en una finestra petita (imatge dins d’imatge) i la teva veu. Revisa el vídeo complet abans de lliurar-lo. Per valorar els hàbits, pregunta’t: aquesta rutina és saludable? Per què sí o per què no? Justifica-ho amb un detall de l’entrevista. Es valora que expliquis la rutina en anglès de manera clara, preparis diapositives útils i revisis el vídeo; no facis comentaris sobre cossos o pes.

Safe

Compare our routines

Main task: Interview a classmate and present their daily routine.

Compare: Compare their routine with your own and explain similarities and differences.

Health reflection: Say whether both routines are healthy and justify your opinion using specific habits from each.

Present Google Slides in a video with your face and slides visible together.

Supporting criteria
  • similarities + differences
  • cause, contrast and consequence
  • natural routine expressions
  • health reflections justified with details from both routines
Daring

Compare, explain & reflect

Main task: Interview a classmate and present their daily routine in depth.

Compare: Compare their routine with your own and explain similarities and differences, using clear evidence and your own interpretation.

Health reflection: Say whether both routines are healthy and justify your opinion using specific habits from each; develop your reasoning and suggest respectful, realistic improvements where appropriate.

Present Google Slides in a video with your face and slides visible together.

Supporting criteria
  • developed comparison
  • richer collocations and connectors
  • justify health judgments about both routines with evidence
  • suggest respectful, realistic improvements where appropriate

Interview → Notes → Google Slides → Prepare → Record presentation → Review → Submit. Share the reviewed video where your teacher indicates; the video must show your face and slides together, with clear sound.

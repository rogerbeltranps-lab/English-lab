4 ESO · Learning Situation

My Real Routine

Use richer, more natural English to describe everyday life, habits, commuting, responsibilities and the way people unwind.

Your challenge

Explain your routine and a friend’s routine naturally

Use richer expressions, connectors and real-life language to describe your own routine and compare it with another person’s.

What will you learn?

You will develop more natural and flexible English for describing your own routine and another person’s routine in real contexts.

  • Natural Present Simple in context
  • Richer routine and commute vocabulary
  • Phrasal verbs and idiomatic expressions
  • Cause, contrast and consequence
  • More developed speaking and writing

How will we work?

Realistic texts, listening, language noticing and progressive production.

  • Listen to authentic-style routines
  • Notice useful chunks
  • Practise shadowing and pronunciation
  • Build richer spoken and written answers
  • Reflect and improve your final version

Grammar & discourse

  • Present Simple in natural contexts
  • Past Simple when relevant
  • Cause: because
  • Contrast: but, although, whereas, however
  • Consequence: so, as a result

Vocabulary & expressions

  • head out, set off, catch up on
  • get down to, knock off
  • rush hour, hit the road
  • bite the bullet, call it a day
  • Evening and unwinding expressions
Final challenge

My routine & my friend’s routine

Create a more developed final product in which you explain your own routine and describe a friend’s routine using accurate language, natural expressions, connectors and personal reflection.

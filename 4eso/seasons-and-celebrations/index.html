Grammar & discourse

  • Present Simple for customs; It is celebrated on… / It takes place in…
  • Prepositions of time: on 31 October, in November, at Christmas
  • Future forms: going to, will, Present Continuous, Present Simple for timetables
  • Origins: Past Simple and used to (Safe, Daring)
  • Relative clauses: which / that, who, where, when (Safe, Daring)
  • Comparing: both… and, whereas, while, however, similar to, different from
  • Formal requests: Could we…? Would it be possible to…?
  • Daring: non-defining relative clauses, Present Simple passive, look forward to + -ing, although / even though

Vocabulary & expressions

  • Seasons, weather and seasonal food: frost, chilly, harvest, chestnuts…
  • Celebrations and customs: festival, tradition, decorations, dress up, light candles…
  • Dates and time: on the night before, once a year, ordinal dates
  • Feelings: excited, grateful, proud…
  • Traditions (Safe): get together, pass down, mark an occasion, exchange gifts
  • Event planning (Safe): set up, run a stall, be held, send invitations
  • Formal email: I am writing to propose… / I look forward to hearing from you
  • Meaning, identity and change (Daring): heritage, commemorate, keep a tradition alive, stereotype, inclusive

📏 Assessment criteria

Each assessment criterion of the curriculum, and how this Learning Situation assesses it: Tap a criterion to see its official wording.

📖 4.1ReadingO1In this LS: Completes a reading log on two texts about celebrations, noting what, when and where each one takes place, its main customs and two words whose meaning they inferred from context.

Curriculum criterion: understand and interpret the overall meaning, structure, key information and purpose of texts, making inferences.

📖 4.2ReadingO2In this LS: Rates each source in the research sheet as reliable or not with at least two reasons (author, date, purpose, type of website or channel) and says which one they trust most.

Curriculum criterion: critically assess the content, quality and reliability of texts and the channel they come from.

🎧 2.2ListeningO1In this LS: After watching two short videos of people describing how they celebrate a festival, explains in a listening chart what each speaker values about it and which words, tone or images show it.

Curriculum criterion: interpret and value the content and features of spoken texts from everyday life, the media and learning.

🗣 3.2SpeakingO3In this LS: Plans, rehearses and records the 2–3 minute group video pitch with the group, sharing out the parts, and explains their part of the celebration (what, when, where, customs, origin) from a script revised so that an audience that does not know it can follow it.

Curriculum criterion: plan, produce, review and cooperate to create coherent texts, using suitable physical or digital resources for the audience.

✏️ 5.1WritingO5In this LS: Writes an individual formal email to the head teacher (Insecure: guided template) that states the purpose, describes the plan and makes a request, using future forms, relative clauses and the conventions of a formal email.

Curriculum criterion: write clear, coherent and appropriate texts of medium length, respecting intellectual property and avoiding plagiarism.

🌍 1.1Intercultural awarenessO3In this LS: Explains in the pitch what the celebration means for the people who celebrate it and why it deserves a place at school, with at least one argument about respect for cultural diversity.

Curriculum criterion: critically value cultural diversity and adapt to it, building a shared culture.

🌍 1.2Intercultural awarenessO4In this LS: Shows in the pitch at least one link between the chosen celebration and a local one (Safe and Daring: similarities and differences) and proposes at least one decision that makes the day inclusive and avoids stereotypes or caricature costumes.

Curriculum criterion: take part respectfully in intercultural situations, building links between cultures and rejecting discrimination, prejudice and stereotypes.

🔎 6.1ResearchO2In this LS: Fills in a research sheet on the chosen celebration with at least two sources (Safe and Daring: three), noting the key information from each source, where they agree or differ, and the full reference.

Curriculum criterion: search, select and contrast information in different sources, judging how reliable and relevant they are.

🤝 CC1 1.3CitizenshipO5In this LS: Includes in the email to the head teacher at least two concrete actions that help every student and family feel welcome on the celebration day (for example, explaining the customs, inviting families or offering an alternative to those who do not celebrate it).

Curriculum criterion: propose actions that help everyone live together.

🤝 CC3 3.2CitizenshipO4In this LS: Names in the pitch at least one stereotype or prejudice about the people who celebrate the festival, explains why it is unfair and shows how the group's plan avoids it (respectful images and words, no caricature costumes).

Curriculum criterion: take an active part in overcoming stereotypes and prejudice.

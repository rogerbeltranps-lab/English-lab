Learning Situation 2 · End of October – December

Seasons & Celebrations

Why do we celebrate, and what can celebrations tell us about people, places and the natural world?

The challenge: a Celebration Day for our school

In groups of 3–4, design a respectful celebration day at school around one tradition. Record a 2–3 minute video pitch with Google Slides (everyone speaks, face and slides visible together) and write a formal email to the head teacher proposing the event.

Final product
Insecure

Describe one celebration: what, when, where, three customs, one food or symbol. Plan three activities. Guided email template.

Safe

Describe it and compare it with a local celebration. Explain its origin. Plan the day with a timetable, roles and materials. Full formal email.

Daring

Explain origin, meaning and change over time. Justify an inclusive event without stereotypes. Answer one concern from the head teacher in the email.

How we learn in this Learning Situation

  • Remember before you look: every Learning Path opens with a recall quiz without notes. Trying to remember is what makes you learn.
  • Again and again: words and grammar come back in later Learning Paths and in weekly recall checkpoints.
  • Mix it up: practice mixes new and old language, so you learn to choose the right form.
  • Feedback and a second try: a step is done when you get 80 % right, so read the feedback and try again.
  • Build your product step by step: each Learning Path gives you one piece of your final product.

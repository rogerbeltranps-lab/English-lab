## Com llegir aquest document

**Fonts.** Mapa vertical de gramàtica, mapa vertical de lèxic, document de nivells i matriu de competències d'anglès (els 4 documents del projecte), la proposta de LS2 i l'exemple de LS1.

**Nivell de cada ruta a 4t** (document de nivells): Insecure = A2 / A2+ amb suport · Safe = B1 orientatiu · Daring = B1 / B1+. Els continguts marcats com a *Ampliació B1+* als mapes només apareixen a Daring.

**Mapes verticals.** Els mapes de gramàtica i de lèxic es respecten per cursos: tot el que marquen per a 4t és en alguna LS, i les LS no introdueixen res que els mapes situïn en un altre curs. Dins de 4t, l'ordre segueix les LS: la columna de 4t dels dos mapes indica ara la LS en lloc de la unitat (U1–U6), i hi consten com a R els sabers de cursos anteriors que les LS recuperen.

**Competències específiques (CE1–CE10).** Codis i criteris d'avaluació de 3r i 4t del currículum oficial de Llengua estrangera (Decret 175/2022), copiats literalment del PDF oficial (carpeta *curriculum* del projecte). Les etiquetes de cada codi (Interculturalitat, Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing, Recerca i informació, Lectura personal, Mediació, Autoregulació, Comunicació ètica) són les de la matriu de competències del projecte. Com a la matriu, a cada LS se separen les habilitats comunicatives (Reading, Listening, Speaking, Writing), que es treballen a totes les LS, de les competències integrades (CE1, CE6–CE10), una o dues per LS.

**Objectius d'aprenentatge.** Cada objectiu segueix l'estructura CAPACITAT + SABER + FINALITAT: un verb de capacitat (equivalent anglès dels verbs de la taula de referència, sobretot dels nivells aplicar, analitzar, avaluar i crear), el saber que es treballa i la finalitat o situació en què es farà servir. Entre parèntesis, la competència específica que concreta.

**Competències transversals.** Codis i criteris d'avaluació de 3r i 4t d'ESO dels documents de l'XTEC sobre competències transversals, copiats literalment: competència ciutadana (CC1–CC4), competència personal, social i d'aprendre a aprendre (CPSAA 1–CPSAA 5), competència digital (CD1–CD5) i competència emprenedora (CE1–CE3). Per distingir les CE de la competència emprenedora de les CE d'anglès, sempre s'hi afegeix *emprenedora*. A cada LS, la línia *Evidència* és una proposta de com es treballa el criteri i no forma part del text oficial.

## LS1 · 4ESO · My Real Routine

*Setembre – octubre · What does a real day look like, and how healthy is it?* Títol i producte final sense canvis; s'hi afegeixen be / have got, canvis d'hàbits, idioms i Listening (CE2).

### 1. Learning objectives

- **Interpret** realistic spoken and written routines, distinguishing evidence from interpretation, in order to extract reliable information about how people really live. (CE2, CE4)

- **Apply** question forms and respectful interaction strategies in an interview with a classmate, in order to gather real details about their routine. (CE3)

- **Describe** routines, commuting and daily responsibilities using Present Simple, be / have got, frequency adverbs and phrasal verbs, in order to present a classmate's real routine in a recorded video. (CE3)

- **Compare** two routines, linking ideas with connectors of cause, contrast and consequence, in order to justify a reflection on healthy habits. Safe and Daring. (CE3)

- **Plan**, write and revise the notes and slides of the presentation, in order to produce a clear, coherent and original multimodal text. (CE5)

- **Assess** their own presentation and a classmate's with a shared checklist, recording strengths and difficulties, in order to plan how to improve their English during the course. (CE9)

### 3. Final Product

Interview → Notes → Google Slides → Prepare → Record presentation → Review → Submit.

Submit a video with Google Slides, your face visible in picture-in-picture and a clear voice. WAV/audio is for short Speaking practice, not the Final Product.

- **Insecure:** Interview and describe the classmate only in short, supported sentences. Reflection: guided judgment and a simple reason using a real interview detail.

- **Safe:** Describe the classmate and compare both routines: similarities and differences. Reflection: justify judgments about both routines with specific habits from each.

- **Daring:** Develop a richer, independent comparison with evidence and interpretation. Reflection: explain judgments about both routines in depth and suggest respectful, realistic improvements where appropriate.

Insecure retains Catalan guidance at every step, with English examples linking interview answer → notes → slide content → spoken sentence → healthy-habit reflection. Discuss habits, not bodies or weight.

### 4. Sabers: Vocabulary

*Cumulative: Safe retains Insecure and adds; Daring retains both and adds.*

**Insecure — foundation**

- Morning: wake up; get up; go to the bathroom; have a shower; have breakfast; listen to the radio

- Day & evening: go to work / school; come home; make dinner; have dinner; phone / call a friend; watch TV; go to bed

- Home & free time: wash clothes; clean the house / your room; go for a walk; write emails / letters; watch a film; go to the cinema; read in bed

- Time, transport & frequency: by bus / train / car; go on foot; walk home; have a cup of tea / coffee; every morning / evening / weekend; two or three times a week; always; usually; often; sometimes; never

- Everyday expressions: head out for school; get home; wind down; turn in; sleep in

- Healthy habits: stay hydrated; get enough sleep; be physically active; take regular breaks; limit screen time; manage stress

**Safe — additions**

- Sleep: wake up / wake someone up; get up early; go to sleep / fall asleep; have an early / late night; get home

- Food: have cereal for breakfast; have a light lunch; have a snack; have a main meal; feed the cat / dog; bother to cook; get / order a takeaway

- Personal care: have a wash / a bath / a shave; clean / brush your teeth; put on make-up

- Housework & errands: do the housework / washing / ironing / shopping; make the bed; go shopping; run errands; stay in; go out; eat out; friends come round; have a chat

- Time & reactions: on weekdays; at the weekend; once a week; straight away; reply straight away

- Expression patterns: wake up at + time; get up at + time; head out for + place; get home at + time; wind down with + thing; wind down by + verb-ing; turn in at + time; sleep in on + day

- Changing habits: cut down on; keep track of; make progress; manage your time; give up; stick to (a plan)

- Healthy habits: maintain a balanced diet; establish a sleep routine; cut down on sugary drinks; take screen breaks; recharge

**Daring — additions**

- MAKE: make arrangements; make a change; make a choice; make a comment; make a decision; make an effort; make an excuse; make friends; make a mistake; make a phone call; make progress

- DO: do your best; do damage; do an experiment; do exercises; do someone a favour; do your hair; do your homework; do the cooking / ironing / shopping / washing; do some work

- TAKE & PAY: take a holiday / trip; take the train / bus; take a risk / chance; take an interest in; take photos; take advantage of; take action; pay attention; pay a compliment; pay tribute

### 5. Sabers: Grammar

**Insecure**

- be i have got per descriure persones i horaris: She's always tired on Mondays. I've got PE on Tuesdays. Have you got a lot of homework?

- Present Simple per a hàbits: I study every afternoon.

- Afirmativa: he/she/it + -s/-es (She works. He watches TV.); have → has, do → does, go → goes.

- Negativa: don't/doesn't + verb base.

- Preguntes i respostes curtes: Do you walk to school? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

- Freqüència: always, usually, often, sometimes, never abans del verb principal i després de be.

- Seqüència i connexió: First, Then, After that, Finally; because, also, but.

- Contrast rutina / fet acabat: Alex usually gets up at seven, but yesterday he slept in.

**Safe** *(retains Insecure and adds)*

- Subject–verb agreement, negative/question forms and frequency placement.

- Phrasal verbs with and without object: I pick up the kids / I wind down / I turn in.

- Separable with noun object: I pick up the kids = I pick the kids up.

- Separable with pronoun object: I pick them up. I put it off.

- Three-part expressions *(B1+ extension)*: I catch up on it. I get down to it.

- Expression patterns: at + time; for + place; with + thing; by + verb-ing; on + day.

- Reasons, comparison, consequence: because, but, although, whereas, however, while, both, so. Present Simple for routines; Past Simple for a specified finished event.

**Daring** *(retains Safe and develops)*

- Object position by pattern: I put it off vs I get down to it.

- Present Simple agreement across longer descriptions; habitual vs finished events.

- Sequence, cause, contrast, consequence: so, as a result, whereas, however…

- After + verb-ing: After whipping up dinner, I get down to work.

- Compare with both and earlier / later; distinguish reported evidence from interpretation.

## LS2 · 4ESO · Seasons & Celebrations

*Finals d'octubre – desembre · Why do we celebrate, and what can celebrations tell us about people, places and the natural world?* Recorregut de la proposta: Read & notice → Practise words → Listen & compare → Research → Create & share → Reflect. Ancoratges de calendari: Castanyada i Halloween (31 d'octubre), Thanksgiving (26 de novembre de 2026), Nadal, i celebracions triades per l'alumnat (Diwali, Hanukkah, Lunar New Year, festes locals…).

### 1. Learning objectives

- **Interpret** written, spoken and multimodal texts about seasons, traditions and celebrations, identifying main ideas, details and meaning from context, in order to build the background knowledge the project needs. (CE2, CE4)

- **Check** information about a celebration in at least two reliable sources and credit them, in order to communicate accurate and respectful content. (CE6)

- **Describe** the customs, dates and origins of a celebration using Present Simple, “it is celebrated / it takes place”, Past Simple and used to, in order to explain it to an audience that does not know it. (CE1, CE3)

- **Compare** the chosen celebration with a local one, valuing diversity and rejecting stereotypes, in order to propose an inclusive school event. Safe and Daring. (CE1)

- **Plan** a school celebration day using future forms (going to, will, Present Continuous) and relative clauses, in order to propose it to the head teacher in a formal email. (CE5)

- Passiva com a chunk: It was shared… It was made… It was taken in…

- Present Simple per a fets: The photo shows a shark on a motorway.

- Connectors: because, so, but, according to.

- Reported statements with backshift: Present Simple → Past Simple, Present Continuous → Past Continuous, Past Simple → Past Perfect, will → would.

- Reported questions: She asked if the photo was real. He asked where it came from.

- Reporting verbs + that: claim, explain, warn, admit, deny that…

- Passive voice, Present and Past Simple, with by + agent: The photo was taken in 2015. Thousands of fake accounts are created every day.

- Modals of deduction (present): It must be fake. It might be real. It can't be true.

**Daring** *(retains Safe and develops)*

- Reporting verb patterns: deny / admit + -ing; warn someone (not) to; advise someone to; refuse to.

- Passive in other forms: It has been shared two million times. It can be checked easily.

- Past deductions *(B1+ extension)*: It must have been edited. He can't have said that.

- Impersonal and hedged statements: It is said that… It seems that…

# 4ESO · LS2 Seasons & Celebrations

Read the [root AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) first; this file adds what is specific to this LS.

- **Content:** [overview.md](overview.md), approved by Roger on 23 Oct 2026 (later approvals are dated in it). The overview numbers the Learning Paths LP0–LP5; on the site they are Learning Paths 1–6, plus 7 (Final product). Programme: [LS2 in the 4ESO programme](../programme.md#ls2--4eso--seasons--celebrations).
- **Final product rubric:** [final-product-assessment.md](final-product-assessment.md): groups, who presents what, individual and group marks, peer assessment and rubrics (draft until Roger approves).
- **Activities and criteria:** [assessment-criteria.md](assessment-criteria.md) maps each activity to the LS2 assessment criteria; [competences-grid.md](competences-grid.md) is the grid by term; [indicators.md](indicators.md) has the NA/AS/AN/AE indicators per LS criterion (Roger's method, step 5), which the rubric descriptors follow.
- **Assessment and grading:** [grading.md](grading.md): the LS mark (exam 35, final product 25, grammar and vocabulary 20, other competences 20), the evidence that gives each criterion its degree (NA/AS/AN/AE) and how the levels are collected. Approved by Roger on 24 Oct 2026.
- **Pages:** `4eso/seasons-and-celebrations/`. Learning Paths 2–6, the checkpoints, the final product, the sample exam and Reflect are thin shells; their content is in `data/<page>.js` and `ls2-engine.js` renders it (recall, vocabulary + flashcards + practice, multiple choice, typed grammar, writing with AI feedback, forms, checklists, speaking, and `step` hand-ins: fields, self-assessment and comment, saved in `state.steps`, shown on the teacher dashboard when the catalogue entry lists them in `steps`; a step can also show media and save a product piece). Learning Path 1 (`getting-started.html`) is a standalone page with five steps: recall, words, reading (*Autumn in two places*, one chat per route), listening (the illustrated origins video in `video/`: `halloween-castanyada.mp4`, ~2:05, and `-slow.mp4` for Insecure, ~2:32, each with a `.vtt`; the real Halloween video is an optional extra) and speaking. The LP3 listening chart uses two more illustrated videos in `video/` (`mei-lunar-new-year`, `noah-hanukkah`; each `.mp4`, `-slow.mp4`, `.vtt`, `-slow.vtt`, `-poster.jpg`), shown by the engine's `media.videos` with a speed toggle.
- **Effective-learning rules used everywhere:** a recall warm-up with no notes opens each Learning Path; earlier vocabulary comes back in the flashcard deck and the recall items; auto-corrected steps complete at 80 %; typed answers show the answer only after a second wrong try; every Learning Path ends with a product piece saved for the Final Product page.
- **Listenings:** pitched at the Safe level (B1) for every route; the questions adapt to each route. Real, level-appropriate videos (Learning Path 2: BBC / British Council "Happy Diwali"; the Fun Easy English "Halloween" video is an optional extra in Learning Path 1) are mixed with scripted listenings generated with ElevenLabs (`audio/*.mp3`, scripts in the data files). All three scripted MP3s exist (podcast, student council meeting, Winter Fair announcement), each with a `-slow` copy; on the Insecure route the engine shows a "Versió més lenta" button that swaps to the slow copy. Until an MP3 exists, the page reads the script with the browser's voices.
- **Readings:** one text per route in every reading (Learning Paths 1, 2, 4, 6 and the sample exam), as in the root rule.
- **Exam listenings** (eleven_v3, voices chosen by Claude at Roger's request, 25 Oct 2026; ~3,950 credits): sample exam `audio/sample-exam-new-year.mp3` (+ `-slow`): Callum = Lewis (Scottish, `gUbIduqGzBP438teh4ZA`; his lines slowed locally to 0.85 because they came out at ~190 words a minute), Yuki = Tonia (`ui11Rd52NKH2DbWlcbvw`). The real exam's recording (Charlotte `6fZce9LFNG3iEITDfqZZ`, Leo Moreno `3l9iCMrNSRR0w51JvFB0`) is only in project files `ls2/exam/audio/`, with clips, gen.py and build.py.
- **ElevenLabs voices for this LS** (Voice Library, model `eleven_v3` with audio tags; chosen by Roger, mixed accents): Emma = Amelia (British, `ZF6FPAbjXT4488VcRRnw`), Sam = Julian (British, `9IzcwKmvwJcw58h3KnlH`), Laia = Zara (American, `jqcCZkN6Knx8BJ5TBdYR`), Omar = Finn (American, `vBKc2FfBKJfcZNyEt1n6`), radio presenter = Clyde (British, `wyWA56cQNU2KqUW4eCsI`). - Recall checkpoints count by completion; the first attempt (score and date) is sent to the dashboard as `parts.first`.
- Activity ids in `el-catalog.js` start with `4eso/ls2-`, and `el-cloud.js` matches pages by path, so they never clash with My Real Routine pages of the same name.

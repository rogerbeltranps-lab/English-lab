# 4ESO · LS2 · Assessment and grading

**Approved by Roger on 24 Oct 2026.** How the ten LS2 criteria are assessed with their indicators and how the LS mark is worked out. The indicators are in [indicators.md](indicators.md); the final product rubric in [final-product-assessment.md](final-product-assessment.md). Discussion doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/25snwnxq9oimsaNVjnQATn. The generic rule is in the root [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md#assessment-and-grading), and this LS is the worked assessment example in Roger's reference doc *Worked example: from competence to LS criterion* (https://claude.ai/artifact/RLZP7UdTgREkiT7MJgCFAR).

## 1. The principle

Every student ends LS2 with a degree on every LS criterion (NA, AS, AN or AE) that the teacher can justify with the indicator and the piece of work it describes. The level comes first; the mark is worked out from it.

Key competences (Roger's *Progressió de les competències d'anglès*, 01 Habilitats comunicatives): CE2 Listening, CE3 Speaking, CE4 Reading, CE5 Writing. In LS2: 4.1, 4.2, 2.2, 3.2, 5.1. Other competences in LS2: 1.1, 1.2, 6.1, CC1 1.3, CC3 3.2.

## 2. The LS mark

| Block | Weight | What it measures | Evidence | How it is marked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Final exam | 35 % | 4.1, 2.2, 5.1, grammar and vocabulary | Google Form, out of 100 (approved 25 Oct 2026): listening 20 (incl. listening chart, 5 multiple choice), reading 20 (incl. reading log, 5 multiple choice), vocabulary 15, grammar 15, writing 30 | Exam score out of 10, marked automatically except the email; the email also gives the 5.1 level |
| Final product | 25 % | 3.2, 5.1, 4.2, individual only | My part of the pitch 40 · my email 35 · my source 15 · teamwork 5 · my process evidence 5 | Rubric levels |
| Grammar and vocabulary | 20 % | Language practice | Vocabulary practice, grammar, mixed practice (LP2, LP3, LP5, LP6), LP6 register step, recall warm-ups, checkpoints 1 and 4 (2 and 3 only if they raise the mark) | Automatic: 60 % first-try accuracy + 40 % completed by the session deadline |
| Other competences | 20 % | 1.1, 1.2, 6.1, CC1 1.3, CC3 3.2 | Every activity linked to them + the final product content rows | Per criterion: half automatic, half teacher levels; the block is the average of the five |

The final product content rows (research card 6.1, meaning 1.1, local link 1.2, stereotypes CC3 3.2, welcome actions CC1 1.3) are still marked with the rubric, but they count in the "other competences" block, not in the 25 %. The 25 % is fully individual. Only the video is marked in groups (Roger, 25 Oct 2026): meaning, local link and stereotypes are judged on the video, once per group; the research card is marked per student.

Key-skill Learning Path hand-ins add no marks of their own; the LP2 reading log and the LP3 listening chart still get a level, as indicator evidence for 4.1 and 2.2.

## 3. Evidence per criterion

| LS criterion | Evidence judged with its indicators | Whose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.1 Reading | LP2 reading log; exam reading | individual |
| 2.2 Listening | LP3 listening chart; exam listening | individual |
| 4.2 Assessing sources | LP4 research card; final product step 1, my source | individual |
| 6.1 Research | LP4 research card; final product research card (round 1) | individual |
| 3.2 Speaking | Final product, my part of the pitch | individual |
| 5.1 Writing | Exam writing; final product email | individual |
| 1.1 Meaning and place at school | Final product, pitch part 1 | group |
| 1.2 Local link and inclusion | Final product, local link and inclusive decision | group |
| CC1 1.3 Welcome actions | Final product email, welcome actions | individual |
| CC3 3.2 Stereotypes | LP4 research card; final product stereotype row | individual, then group |

Rules:

- Where there are two pieces of evidence, the later one weighs more. The dashboard suggests the degree; the teacher confirms or changes it.
- A percentage of right answers never sets the degree for 4.1 or 2.2 on its own: their indicators describe a task (reading log, listening chart). In the exam the listening chart and the reading log are multiple choice (Roger, 25 Oct 2026), so the exam gives a listening and a reading score, not a level: the 2.2 and 4.1 degrees rest on the LP3 chart and LP2 log levels, with the exam scores as supporting evidence.
- 1.1 and 1.2 rest on group evidence; it becomes an individual degree through peer assessment and the teacher's observation (safety net: one level less for a student who did not contribute). Option not yet decided: also level 1.1 on the final email.

## 4. Other competences: the two halves

| Criterion | Automatic half: LP steps linked to it | Teacher half: levels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | LP2 speaking handed in | Final product, pitch part 1 (group) |
| 1.2 | LP3 writing and speaking handed in; LP4 respectful wording, first try | Final product, local link and inclusion (group) |
| 6.1 | LP4 notes and credit line, first try; research card handed in | Research card; final product research card (round 1) |
| CC1 1.3 | LP5 plan and LP6 email handed in | Final product, welcome actions |
| CC3 3.2 | LP4 respectful wording, first try | Research card stereotype; final product stereotype row (group) |

Automatic half: share of linked steps done, auto-corrected steps counting their first-try score, as a mark out of 10. Teacher half: the level (or the average of two levels). Missing work counts 0 until handed in.

## 5. Collecting the data

The indicators are not ticked one by one: each row of indicators.md is a four-level scale, so recording it is one click, the level whose description fits best.

| Moment | Levels per student | Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LP2 reading log | 1 | 4.1 |
| LP3 listening chart | 1 | 2.2 |
| LP4 research card | 3 | 4.2, 6.1, CC3 3.2 |
| Exam: writing | 1 | 5.1 |
| Final product round 1 (after step 2): my source, my research card | 2 | 4.2 · 6.1 |
| Final product round 2 (after submitting): my part, my email, welcome actions; meaning, local link, stereotypes once per group (on the video) | 3 + 3 per group | 3.2, 5.1, CC1 1.3 · 1.1, 1.2, CC3 3.2 |

About 300 clicks for a class of 25 over six weeks. Not levelled: LP writing pieces (AI feedback), LP speaking practice, self-assessment (shown next to the teacher's level, never counted).

Levels to marks: NA 2.5 · AS 5 · AN 7.5 · AE 10 · no evidence 0. Marks to levels for the report: under 5 NA · 5–6.9 AS · 7–8.9 AN · 9–10 AE.

## 7. Terms

Term 1 (evaluation sessions 2 and 9 Dec): exam, grammar and vocabulary, LP evidence. The final product (deadline 11 Dec) closes the LS2 mark in term 2. It is marked in two rounds (Roger, 25 Oct 2026): round 1 after step 2 (my source, my research card), round 2 after submitting (video, email, teamwork, process), each with a marking PDF of every student's answers ([final-product-assessment.md](final-product-assessment.md#8-marking-in-two-rounds-roger-25-oct-2026)).

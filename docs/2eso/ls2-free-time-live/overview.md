# 2ESO · Free Time, Live! (LS2): activity content

**25–26 Oct 2026.** Built by Claude from the [2ESO programme](../programme.md#ls2--2eso--free-time-live) with 4ESO LS2 as the model, under Roger's standing permission (decisions in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)). The content lives in `2eso/free-time-live/data/<page>.js`; this file is the readable map. Routes: Insecure (A1+/A2 inicial, amb bastides; Catalan guidance) · Safe (A2 funcional) · Daring (A2+/B1 emergent). Routes are cumulative: Safe sees everything Insecure sees plus its own items, Daring sees all three.

## 0. Rules that apply to every page

- A recall warm-up with no notes opens each Learning Path (LS1 and earlier Learning Paths); the first try is sent to the dashboard.
- Vocabulary comes in thematic groups (word · Catalan · example) with flashcards and a practice quiz (80 % to complete); earlier words come back in the review deck.
- Readings have one text per route; listenings and videos are the same for every route (Safe level), with questions per route and a free slow copy for Insecure.
- Typed grammar and Mixed practice: one gap per item, the answer shows after the second wrong try.
- Each Learning Path ends with a writing that saves a **product piece** (`ls2Products2ESO`) reused on the final product page, and a speaking mini-challenge (record, transcript, AI feedback).
- Steps that count for the grammar and vocabulary block show the yellow "first try counts" note.

## LP1 · Getting started: what do you do in your free time?

- **Recall** (LS1 My Real Routine): daily routine verbs, Present Simple, frequency.
- **Vocabulary:** play + sport/game · go + -ing · do + activity · other activities; Safe: *How often?*; Daring: talking about hobbies. Review deck from LS1.
- **Reading:** *Two posts from Saturday* (Marta at the skate park · Hugo at home), one version per route; questions on what they are doing now and what they usually do.
- **Listening:** *Where are you?* (`audio/where-are-you.mp3`, Laia and Omar on the phone, about 50 s): where each one is, what people are doing, what they usually do on Saturdays.
- **Speaking:** say three things you do in your free time and one thing you are doing now.

## LP2 · What are they doing? (criterion 4.1)

- **Recall** (LP1). **Vocabulary:** clothes and kit · weather; Safe: events, more weather; Daring: at the event.
- **Reading:** *A post and a web page* (Climb Up Club post · Autumn Fun Run page), per route.
- **Reading log (4.1, step `log`, hand-in):** for each text, who/what is happening, where and when, what people usually do, the purpose of the text (to inform, to invite, to sell); Safe adds a word inferred from context; Daring compares the two texts.
- **Listening:** *Live from the sports hall* (`audio/live-from-the-sports-hall.mp3`, Emma and Sam, about 80 s): a live report from a volleyball tournament.
- **Grammar:** Present Continuous (affirmative, negative, questions, short answers) and -ing spelling. **Mixed practice.**
- **Writing → product piece 1:** *In this photo…* (describe a photo of a hobby or event: what people are doing, what they are wearing, the weather; Insecure with sentence frames).
- **Speaking:** describe what people around you are doing right now.

## Recall checkpoint 1 (after LP2; counts)

Items from LS1, LP1 and LP2 (vocabulary, Present Continuous, -ing spelling). JSON data, first try sent as `parts.first`.

## LP3 · Now or usually? (criterion 2.2)

- **Recall** (LP1–2). **Vocabulary:** time expressions for now and usually; Safe: doing sport; Daring: state verbs.
- **Listening:** video 1 *Mei: Saturday at the ice rink* (`video/mei-ice-rink`, about 48 s) with multiple-choice questions.
- **Listening chart (2.2, step `chart`, hand-in):** both videos (`video/mei-ice-rink`, `video/noah-bike-park`): for each vlogger, what they are doing now, what they usually do, two details (place, clothes or weather); Safe adds how they knew (words or images); Daring compares the two vloggers.
- **Grammar:** Present Simple vs Present Continuous with time expressions; temporary situations (*This month I'm…*); state verbs (*think, like, know, prefer*). **Mixed practice.**
- **Writing → product piece 2:** *I usually… but today…* (Insecure: three contrast sentences with frames; Safe: a short paragraph; Daring: adds a temporary situation).
- **Speaking:** usually vs today about one hobby.

## Recall checkpoint 2 (after LP3; optional)

Items from LP1–3.

## LP4 · Check the source (criteria 4.2 and 6.2; term 2, after the exam)

- **Recall** (LP1–3, with the vocabulary of events and weather). **Vocabulary:** technology; Safe: online; Daring: sources.
- **Reading:** *Can you trust this page?* (a checklist page: author, date, purpose, type of website), per route.
- **Sources (multiple choice):** six short page descriptions rated reliable or not, with the reason shown (`ex`).
- **Credits (multiple choice):** how to credit a photo and a web page on the last slide (title, website, year; photo: who took it).
- **Fact file (4.2 + 6.2, step `factfile`, hand-in = product piece 3):** topic; source 1 rated with two reasons; Safe and Daring: source 2 rated and the one chosen; four facts in key words; Daring: where the sources agree or differ; photos and who took them. Insecure works from a given source ("Skateboarding", Encyclopedia website, 2026).

## LP5 · Likes and screen tips (criterion CD4 4.5)

- **Recall** (LP1–3). **Vocabulary:** likes and dislikes (+ -ing) · screen tips (imperatives); Safe: healthy habits; Daring: why it matters.
- **Listening:** *Screen smart* (`audio/screen-smart.mp3`, a radio presenter, about 85 s): tips for a healthy use of screens.
- **Grammar:** *love / like / don't like / hate + -ing*; imperatives (*Take a break. Don't use your phone in bed.*); Safe: *because / so*; Daring: *instead of + -ing*. **Mixed practice.**
- **Writing → product piece 4:** my screen tips (Insecure two, Safe three, Daring three with a reason each, and the one I will apply).
- **Speaking:** say what you love and hate doing, and one tip.

## Recall checkpoint 3 (after LP5; optional)

Items from LP1–3 and LP5 (no LP4 item: LP4 comes after the exam).

## LP6 · Write your vlog script (criterion 5.1)

- **Recall** (LP1–5). **Vocabulary:** vlog phrases; Safe: hobbies and commitment; Daring: opinions with state verbs. Review deck LP1–5.
- **Reading:** *Noa's climbing vlog*, the model script in seven slides (greeting · live now · usually · photo or facts · likes · tips · credits and goodbye), one version per route; **Parts** (multiple choice): which part says what.
- **Grammar:** review of all the forms of the vlog (Present Continuous vs Simple, -ing, like + -ing, imperatives; Safe: *and, but, because, so*; Daring: opinions with state verbs).
- **Writing → product piece 5:** the full vlog script (Insecure 90–120 words with a template, Safe 130–170, Daring 170–220), seven parts, AI feedback, saved for the final product.
- **Speaking:** rehearse the script aloud and record it.

## LP7 · Final product: Free Time Vlog

See [final-product-assessment.md](final-product-assessment.md). ## Reflect

Reads the four checkpoints' first tries and asks five reflection questions (what I can do now, what was hard, my best piece, my route, the screen tip I applied and how it is going).

## Beginners route (A1), added 26 Oct 2026

Beginners sits below Insecure (AGENTS.md, "Beginners"). Everything untagged in the data files is for every route; items tagged `r: 'insecure'` start at Insecure; a text, task, frame, list or rubric with no `beginners` key falls back to the Insecure one. What Beginners gets in this LS:

- **Readings of 60–100 words with a Catalan word line** (`media.text.beginners`): the two Saturday posts (LP1), the Climb Up Club post and the Fun Run page (LP2), the "can you trust this page?" text (LP4), Noa's model script (LP6) and the sample-exam texts. Every question Beginners sees is answerable from its text.
- **Fewer, easier questions:** 5–6 recall items per page (the LS1 ones and the core words), 5 reading and 5 listening questions with the Catalan hints, grammar with affirmative forms and -ing spelling only (negatives, questions, short answers and state verbs start at Insecure), easy mixed-practice items.
- **Lighter steps:** reading log on text A only plus where/when of text B (LP2); listening chart on Mei's video only (LP3); fact file with two facts from the given source (LP4), each with its own Catalan self-assessment.
- **Writing and speaking:** frames with fewer gaps and a word bank (piece 1 35–50 words, piece 2 40–55, piece 4 35–50, script 60–80), speaking 15–30 s with a short model.
- **Final product:** a Beginners paragraph and requirements list on the task tab (seven short slides from the frame, one *is / are + -ing* and one *usually* sentence, two facts, two tips from the model, 1–1.5 minutes), checklists and self-assessments; its own rubric (`finalRubricData.beginners`: vlog, script, fact file and tips rows; source, process and editing shared with Insecure). The model page is the Insecure one for now.
- **Checkpoints:** 5–6 items tagged `beginners` each (checkpoint 1: 5, 2: 5, 3: 6, 4: 6). **Sample exam:** `items.beginners` for every section (same number of items as the other routes) and a script template with a word bank (40–60 words).

## Four skills (30 Oct 2026)

Roger (30 Oct 2026, “very important”): the learning paths work listening, speaking, reading and writing equally; the exam and the sample exam do not change. The full texts (questions, options, hints, tasks and models per route) are in the data files (`2eso/free-time-live/data/<page>.js`, panels `again` and `talk`). No new audio was generated: every *Listen again* uses a recording the LS already has.

- **LP1 stays exactly as it was** (Roger, 30 Oct 2026: students had already done it), so the changes are in LP2 to LP6, within their planned classes (`el-plan.js`).
- **Use of English heard or said:** practice is *Listen and complete* in LP2 to LP6, and grammar in LP2, LP3 and LP5; mixed practice is *Say it* in LP2, LP3 and LP5, and the LP6 grammar is *Say it*. The study cards already ask students to say each word aloud before turning the card, so from LP2 on they count as reading and speaking.
- **LP2 What are they doing?** *Listen again: what are they doing?* (the LP1 call “Where are you?”; where they are and what they are doing now; Safe/Daring: usually vs now). *Talk: video call*: where are you, what are you doing, who are you with (+ weather; Safe: what people around you are doing, what you are wearing; Daring: is this what you usually do).
- **LP3 Now or usually?** *Talk: now or usually?*: what you usually do on Saturdays, what you are doing this weekend, sport (+ who with; Safe: how often, why this weekend is different; Daring: which you prefer).
- **LP4 Check the source.** *Listen: facts or opinions?* (the LP2 sports hall radio; who speaks, where, a fact, an opinion, numbers; Safe/Daring: “about”, an unfinished match, what a credit needs). *Talk: where did you find it?*: which website, who wrote it, is it good (+ new or old; Safe: one fact, the credit in the vlog; Daring: checking on a second website).
- **LP5 Likes and screen tips.** *Listen again: Noah’s Saturday* (Noah’s bike park vlog from LP3; where, who with, weather, usually vs today; Safe/Daring: who the video is for, likes, using a screen outdoors, the Screen Smart tip it follows). *Talk: free time and screens*: a School Radio interview about what you love and hate doing and screen time (+ best tip; Safe: phone before bed, outdoor fun; Daring: is 3.5 hours a day too much).
- **LP6 Write your vlog script.** *Listen again: how a vlog starts and ends* (Mei’s ice rink vlog from LP3; greeting, where she is, what is happening, usually, goodbye; Safe/Daring: usually vs today, “Look!”, an idea to copy). *Talk: interview about your vlog*: what it is about, where you are, why you like it (+ who with; Safe: what is happening, how you start and end; Daring: your tip for viewers).

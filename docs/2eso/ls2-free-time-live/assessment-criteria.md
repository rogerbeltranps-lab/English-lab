# 2ESO · LS2 · Activities and assessment criteria

**25 Oct 2026.** Each activity of *Free Time, Live!* is linked to the LS criteria from the [2ESO programme](../programme.md#ls2--2eso--free-time-live) by the skill it practises and the competence its content targets. Tags live in `shared/js/el-catalog.js` (`EL_ACTIONS`, `EL_STEP_CRITERIA`); indicators in [indicators.md](indicators.md); how the marks add up in [grading.md](grading.md).

## The eight LS2 criteria

| Criterion | Objective | LS assessment criterion (task) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **4.1** Reading | O1 | Reading log on two posts or web pages: what people are doing, where and when, what they usually do, the purpose of each text. |
| **4.2** Reading (sources) | O2 | Rates two pages about the chosen hobby or event as reliable or not with two reasons from the checklist (author, date, purpose, type of website) and chooses one. |
| **2.2** Listening | O1 | Listening chart on two short vlogs: what each vlogger is doing, what they usually do, two details (place, clothes, weather), using images, key words and context. |
| **3.2** Speaking | O3 | Plans, records and revises the "live" vlog (Google Slides + face in picture-in-picture), contrasting now and usually; improves one part after the first recording. |
| **5.1** Writing | O4 | Writes the vlog script and slide text following a model: time expressions, likes and dislikes with -ing, screen tips with imperatives (Insecure two, Safe three, Daring three with a reason). |
| **6.2** Research | O2 | Organises the information in a fact file (Insecure: four facts from one given source; Safe and Daring: two sources) and credits photos and sources on the last slide. |
| **CD2 2.2** Digital | O3 | Records and edits the vlog with guidance: cuts at least one mistake or long pause; each photo and caption with its part of the script. |
| **CD4 4.5** Digital | O4 | Screen tips that prevent addictive habits (time limits, breaks, no phone before sleeping) and the one they will apply. |

**Four skills (Roger, 30 Oct 2026).** LP1 and LP2 stay as they were (students had started them). From LP3 on the learning paths also have: *Listen again* (`again` → 2.2) in LP4, LP5 and LP6; *Talk* (`talk` → 3.2, a hand-in the teacher sees) in LP3–LP6; practice heard in LP3–LP6 and grammar heard in LP3 and LP5 (*Listen and complete* → 2.2, 5.1); mixed practice said (*Say it* → 3.2; LP6: grammar said); study cards said aloud (4.1, 3.2). Other vocabulary and grammar steps count for 4.1 and 5.1 (Use of English is reading and writing).

## Criterion by criterion

- **4.1:** LP2 reading log (level) + exam reading (score). **2.2:** LP3 listening chart (level) + exam listening (score). **4.2:** LP4 fact file + final product step 1 (levels; the later one weighs twice). **6.2:** LP4 fact file + final product fact file and credits (levels) + automatic half (LP4 Credits, fact file done). **3.2:** final product step 4 / the video (level). **5.1:** exam writing (level) + final product step 3 (level). **CD2 2.2:** step 4 (level) + automatic half (steps 2 and 4 done). **CD4 4.5:** step 5 (level) + automatic half (LP5 writing and speaking, step 5 done).

## Terms

Term 1 (to 20 Nov): 4.1, 2.2, 5.1 (exam), grammar and vocabulary, CD4 4.5 (LP5). Term 2 (to 11 Dec): 4.2, 6.2, 3.2, CD2 2.2, 5.1 (final product), CD4 4.5 (step 5), process.

# 2ESO · LS2 · Final product: the Free Time Vlog

**25 Oct 2026.** How the vlog is made and marked. Roger's choice (decision card, 25 Oct 2026): **individual vlog, pairs help**. Page: `2eso/free-time-live/free-time-vlog.html` (`data/free-time-vlog.js`); rubric: `final-rubric-data.js`.

## 1. Who makes what

- Every student makes **their own 1.5–2 minute vlog**: a video with Google Slides and their face in picture-in-picture, clear voice, about a hobby or a free-time event. Audio alone is only for speaking practice.
- The teacher pairs **vlog buddies** of the same route. Buddies research together, help each other with the slides and give feedback on each other's first recording (step "Buddy feedback", not marked). Nothing is marked in pairs: every rubric row is individual, so there are no group rows and no peer rows.
- Process: choose a hobby or event → research (my source) → script → Google Slides → record → watch the first recording and improve a part → submit on Classroom.

## 2. What each route asks for

| | Insecure | Safe | Daring |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slides | 7, with a photo and a caption each: greeting · now · usually · photo · likes · tips · credits | 7: greeting · live now · usually · photo · likes · three tips · credits | 7: greeting · live now · usually + this month · facts · likes and opinions · three tips with reasons · credits |
| Language | Present Continuous for the photos, Present Simple for usually, short supported sentences (Catalan guidance, frames *In this photo, they're… / They usually…*) | now vs usually with time expressions; weather and clothes or kit; *love / like / hate + -ing*; imperatives, one negative | adds a temporary situation (*This month I'm training for…*), opinions with state verbs (*I think, I prefer… to…*), *and, but, because, so* |
| Research | four facts from one given source ("Skateboarding", Encyclopedia website, 2026) | two sources rated with two reasons, one chosen | two sources rated, compared, both credited |
| Tips | two screen tips with imperatives | three | three with a reason each, plus the one they will apply |
| Editing | face and slides visible; one mistake or long pause cut | + each photo with its caption and script part; one part improved after the first recording | + good rhythm, more than one part improved on their own |

Each step has self-assessment statements (1–4) and an optional comment; they do not change the mark by themselves.

## 4. How it is marked

Final product block (25 % of the LS mark): **My vlog 40** (3.2, step 4 + video) · **My script and slides 30** (5.1, step 3) · **My source 15** (4.2, step 1) · **My process evidence 15** (steps 0–5). Other competences rows on the rubric, counted in the 20 % block: My fact file and credits (6.2), Recording and editing (CD2 2.2), Screen tips (CD4 4.5). Levels NA/AS/AN/AE per row, descriptors per route from [indicators.md](indicators.md).

## 5. Marking in two rounds

- **Round 1 · Source and fact file** (after step 2, before recording): LP4 fact file, steps 0–2 → levels 4.2 and 6.2. Students get the levels and can fix the source or the facts before recording.
- **Round 2 · Vlog, script, editing and tip** (after submitting, 14–18 Dec 2026): steps 3–5 with the self-ratings, the process, and the video on Classroom → levels 5.1, 3.2, CD2 2.2, CD4 4.5, process.
- One marking PDF per class per round (each rubric row right below the activity it marks), to be built in the English Lab project as for 4ESO.

## 6. Model vlogs

The page opens with the route's model, a fictional student: Júlia · swimming (Insecure, Catalan guidance), Arnau · basketball (Safe), Lia · hip-hop dance (Daring). Each model is the seven slides plus what the student says on each one, written to match the rubric; students use it for ideas and choose a different hobby or event.

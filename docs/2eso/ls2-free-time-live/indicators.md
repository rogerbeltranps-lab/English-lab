# 2ESO · LS2 · Achievement indicators (NA · AS · AN · AE)

**25 Oct 2026.** Step 5 of Roger's method (*Worked example: from competence to LS criterion*): for each LS assessment criterion of *Free Time, Live!*, four indicators that say how well the objective has been achieved. Steps 1–4 are in the [programme](../programme.md#ls2--2eso--free-time-live). Written by Claude under Roger's standing permission; the same texts are in `shared/js/el-grading.js` (`EL_GRADING["2eso-free-time-live"].ind`, the dashboard shows them on hover) and this table is generated from them: change them there and regenerate.

- **How the levels differ:** the help the student needs (model, template, checklist, subtitles or a second viewing), the complexity of what they do, and its quality.
- **How many times (Roger, 10 Nov 2026).** Each level keeps the quality and the help it described and adds how many times the student shows the indicator, with the same cuts as the mark: fewer than half = NA, half = AS, 7 in 10 = AN, 9 in 10 = AE (the mark out of 10 is the % of times the student showed what the indicators describe). For the tips, the number asked for by route does the same job. A level needs both the number and the quality.
- **One set for all routes.** Routes change the task and the help (Catalan guidance, templates, one given source or two sources, number of tips), not the indicators.
- **On the final product rubric:** NA = Level 1 (2.5) · AS = Level 2 (5) · AN = Level 3 (7.5) · AE = Level 4 (10). The descriptors in `2eso/free-time-live/final-rubric-data.js` follow these indicators, written for each route.
- **Where the evidence is:** see [assessment-criteria.md](assessment-criteria.md).

| **4.1** Reading log on two texts (O1) | Fewer than half of the facts asked for are right (what people are doing, where and when, what they usually do, the purpose), or whole sentences copied; no purpose given, even with the model. | At least half of the facts right, close to the model and often copied; the purpose of at least one text. | 7 in 10 facts right, in short notes of their own; the purpose of each text with a word from the text that shows it. | 9 in 10 facts right, without help; infers a new word from context and compares how the two texts use language. |
| **4.2** Rating two sources (O2) | Fewer than half of the reasons right, or an obviously unreliable page (a shop, an anonymous video) rated as reliable, even with the checklist. | At least half of the reasons right, copied from the checklist without really looking at the page; one page chosen. | 7 in 10 reasons right and relevant (author, date, purpose, type of website); the better page chosen. | 9 in 10 reasons right, precise and found on their own, including the purpose (selling, getting views, informing) and the type of website; explains the choice. |
| **2.2** Listening chart on two vlogs (O1) | Fewer than half of the boxes right (for each vlogger: what they are doing now, what they usually do and two details), or only the place, even after a second viewing with subtitles. | At least half of the boxes right: what each vlogger is doing now or usually does, plus one detail, with subtitles or a second viewing. | 7 in 10 boxes right: what they are doing now, what they usually do and two details (place, clothes or weather), using the images and key words. | 9 in 10 boxes right, with precise details; says which words or images helped and compares the two vloggers. |
| **3.2** The vlog: plan, record, revise (O3) | Does not record the vlog, or reads more than half of the text and the audience cannot follow it; face and slides not visible. | Records the vlog with guidance and covers the basic content (where, what people are doing, likes); reads up to half of the text; at least half of the now / usually sentences without errors. | Plans and records with Slides and face in picture-in-picture; reads at most 2 sentences; 7 in 10 now (Present Continuous) and usually (Present Simple) sentences without errors; improves one part after the first recording. | A fluent, well-structured vlog with a good rhythm, 1.5–2 minutes; does not read; 9 in 10 sentences without errors and a natural contrast; improves more than one part on their own. |
| **5.1** Vlog script and slide text (O4) | Unfinished or not understandable; no clear parts, even with the template; fewer than half of the verbs (Present Continuous and Present Simple) right. | The parts are there, close to the template or model; at least half of the verbs right; errors that do not block meaning; the tips are imperatives. | The seven parts of the model, adapted; 7 in 10 verbs right, and time expressions, like / love / hate + -ing and imperatives used correctly; short slide text. | A clear, well-linked script beyond the model (and, but, because, so; Plus: a temporary situation and an opinion); 9 in 10 forms right; corrects their own errors. |
| **6.2** Fact file and credits (O2) | Fewer than half of the facts asked for, copied sentences or wrong information; no credits. | Half of the facts, with help and partly copied; at least half of the credits (sources and photos), some incomplete. | 3 in 4 facts right, in key words from the required sources (Base: one given source; Core and Plus: two); 7 in 10 credits complete on the last slide. | All the facts accurate and in their own words, from well-chosen sources they compared; every credit complete, on their own. |
| **CD2 2.2** Recording and editing (O3) | No editing: the mistakes and long pauses are left, or fewer than half of the photos are with their part of the script. | Records with guidance; a mistake or long pause is left; at least half of the photos with their part of the script. | Records with Slides and face in picture-in-picture; cuts at least one mistake or long pause; 7 in 10 photos with their caption and their script part. | Records and edits on their own: no mistakes or long pauses left, good rhythm; 9 in 10 photos with their caption and their part. |
| **CD4 4.5** Screen tips (O4) | No tips, fewer than half of the tips asked for (Base two, Core and Plus three), or tips that are not about screens. | At least half of the tips asked for, general and close to the model; does not say which one they will apply. | All the tips asked for, with imperatives, that prevent addictive habits (time limits, breaks, no phone before sleeping); says which one they will apply. | All the tips asked for, each with a reason; explains the one they are applying and how it is going. |
| **process** Process evidence (steps 0–5) | Fewer than 3 of the 6 steps (0–5) handed in. | 3 or 4 of the 6 steps handed in. | 5 of the 6 steps handed in, or all 6 with one late or very general. | All 6 steps handed in on time, specific and in their own words, with an honest self-assessment. |

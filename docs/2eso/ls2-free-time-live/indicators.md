# 2ESO · LS2 · Achievement indicators (NA · AS · AN · AE)

**25 Oct 2026.** Step 5 of Roger's method (*Worked example: from competence to LS criterion*): for each LS assessment criterion of *Free Time, Live!*, four indicators that say how well the objective has been achieved. Steps 1–4 are in the [programme](../programme.md#ls2--2eso--free-time-live). Written by Claude under Roger's standing permission; the same texts are in `shared/js/el-grading.js` (`EL_GRADING["2eso-free-time-live"].ind`, the dashboard shows them on hover) and this table is generated from them: change them there and regenerate.

- **How the levels differ:** the help the student needs (model, template, checklist, subtitles or a second viewing), the complexity of what they do, and its quality.
- **One set for all routes.** Routes change the task and the help (Catalan guidance, templates, one given source or two sources, number of tips), not the indicators.
- **On the final product rubric:** NA = Level 1 (2.5) · AS = Level 2 (5) · AN = Level 3 (7.5) · AE = Level 4 (10). The descriptors in `2eso/free-time-live/final-rubric-data.js` follow these indicators, written for each route.
- **Where the evidence is:** see [assessment-criteria.md](assessment-criteria.md).

| LS criterion (task) | NA · No assoliment | AS · Assoliment satisfactori | AN · Assoliment notable | AE · Assoliment excel·lent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **4.1** Reading log on two texts (O1) | The log is unfinished or mostly wrong, even with the model: what people are doing is missing for one text, sentences copied, no purpose given. | Both texts logged with what people are doing, where and when and what they usually do, close to the model and often copied; the purpose named for at least one text. | Both texts logged accurately in short notes of their own, with the purpose of each text and a word from the text that shows it. | Accurate, concise log without help; infers a new word from context and compares how the two texts use language. |
| **4.2** Rating two sources (O2) | No reasons, or an obviously unreliable page (a shop, an anonymous video) rated as reliable, even with the checklist. | Each page rated with one reason, or with reasons copied from the checklist without looking at the page; one page chosen. | Both pages rated correctly with two relevant reasons from the checklist (author, date, purpose, type of website); the better one chosen. | Rates the pages on their own with precise reasons, including the purpose (selling, getting views, informing) and the type of website, and explains the choice. |
| **2.2** Listening chart on two vlogs (O1) | Chart missing, or only the place is given, even after a second viewing with subtitles. | For each vlogger, what they are doing now or what they usually do, plus one detail, with subtitles or a second viewing. | For each vlogger, what they are doing now, what they usually do and two details (place, clothes or weather), using the images and key words. | Complete chart with precise details, says which words or images helped, and compares the two vloggers. |
| **3.2** The vlog: plan, record, revise (O3) | Does not record the vlog, or reads a text the audience cannot follow; face and slides not visible. | Records the vlog with guidance and covers the basic content (where, what people are doing, likes), but the contrast now / usually is weak, reads parts, or changed nothing after the first recording. | Plans and records the vlog with Slides and face in picture-in-picture; contrasts now (Present Continuous) and usually (Present Simple); improves one part after watching the first recording; only glances at the notes. | A fluent, well-structured vlog with a good rhythm; the contrast is clear and natural; improves more than one part on their own; 1.5–2 minutes. |
| **5.1** Vlog script and slide text (O4) | Unfinished or not understandable; no clear parts, even with the template; no time expressions or tips. | The parts are there, close to the template or model; Present Continuous and Present Simple mostly correct; errors that do not block meaning; the tips are imperatives. | The seven parts of the model, adapted: time expressions, like / love / hate + -ing and tips with imperatives used correctly; at most 2–3 errors; short slide text. | A clear, well-linked script beyond the model (and, but, because, so; Daring: a temporary situation and an opinion), few errors, corrected on their own. |
| **6.2** Fact file and credits (O2) | Fewer than four facts, copied sentences, no credits, or wrong information. | Four facts with help, partly copied; a credit line incomplete, or a photo without a credit. | Four facts in key words from the required sources (Insecure: one given source; Safe and Daring: two), with full credit lines; photos and sources credited on the last slide. | Four accurate facts in their own words from well-chosen sources that they compared; everything credited completely, on their own. |
| **CD2 2.2** Recording and editing (O3) | No editing: mistakes and long pauses are left, or the photos do not match the script. | Records with guidance; one mistake or long pause is left, or a photo is not with its part of the script. | Records with Slides and face in picture-in-picture, cuts at least one mistake or long pause, and places each photo with its caption and its script part. | Records and edits on their own: no mistakes or long pauses left, good rhythm, every photo with its caption and its part. |
| **CD4 4.5** Screen tips (O4) | No tips, or tips that are not about screens. | Two or three general tips, close to the model, without saying which one they will apply. | Tips with imperatives that prevent addictive habits (time limits, breaks, no phone before sleeping; Insecure two, Safe three, Daring three with a reason), and the one they will apply. | Three tips with reasons that prevent addictive habits, and explains the one they are applying and how it is going. |
| **process** Process evidence (steps 0–5) | Most steps are missing. | One or two steps are missing. | Every step handed in; one is late or very general. | Every step handed in on time, specific and in their own words, with an honest self-assessment. |

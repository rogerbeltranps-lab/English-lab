# 2ESO · LS2 Free Time, Live!

Read the [root AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) first; this file adds what is specific to this LS. Built on 25–26 Oct 2026 as a copy of 4ESO LS2 (the living model), following the [new LS playbook](../../general/new-ls-playbook.md). Roger's standing permission for this LS (25 Oct 2026): do everything that was done for 4ESO LS2 without asking, take the recommended option on every content or structure choice, and list the decisions taken for him (decision log below).

- **Programme:** [LS2 in the 2ESO programme](../programme.md#ls2--2eso--free-time-live) (Roger's *Learning Situations 2n ESO*, 21 Oct 2026, rev. 24 Oct). Driving question *What do we really do in our free time?* · late October to December · Present Continuous vs Present Simple, free time, events and weather, guided research and healthy screen use.
- **Content:** [overview.md](overview.md) (Learning Paths 1–6, checkpoints, sample exam, final product, Reflect). Readings vary per route; listenings and videos are at Safe level for every route with questions per route.
- **Criteria and indicators:** [assessment-criteria.md](assessment-criteria.md) maps each activity to the eight LS criteria (4.1, 4.2, 2.2, 3.2, 5.1, 6.2, CD2 2.2, CD4 4.5); [indicators.md](indicators.md) has the NA/AS/AN/AE indicators, also in `shared/js/el-grading.js` (`ind`): keep them in step; [competences-grid.md](competences-grid.md) is the grid by term.
- **Grading:** [grading.md](grading.md): exam 35 · final product 25 · grammar and vocabulary 20 · other competences 20 (Roger's weights), evidence per criterion, moments for one-click levels.
- **Final product:** [final-product-assessment.md](final-product-assessment.md): the Free Time Vlog is **individual** (Roger, 25 Oct 2026), with a same-route vlog buddy for research and feedback; two marking rounds; rubric in `2eso/free-time-live/final-rubric-data.js`.
- **Pages:** `2eso/free-time-live/` (green 2ESO palette, `lp-theme-2eso.css`). Every page, Learning Path 1 included, is a thin shell on `ls2-engine.js` (a copy of the 4ESO engine with the 2ESO page list, product key `ls2Products2ESO`, LS id `2eso-free-time-live` and Catalan prompts for 2n d'ESO); content in `data/<page>.js`. Order in the page grid and the menu: 1, 2, C1, 3, C2, 4, 5, C3, 6, C4, sample exam, 7, Reflect. Model vlogs: `insecure-model.html`, `safe-model.html`, `daring-model.html` (fictional students Júlia · swimming, Arnau · basketball, Lia · hip-hop; slides + what they say; build scripts in project files `ls2-2eso/final-product-models/`).
- **Audio and video** (all eleven_v3 with audio tags, voices approved by Roger for 4ESO LS2; scripts, clips and build scripts in project files `ls2-2eso/`): `audio/where-are-you` (Laia = Zara, Omar = Finn), `audio/live-from-the-sports-hall` (Emma = Amelia, Sam = Julian), `audio/screen-smart` (Presenter = Clyde), `audio/sample-exam-two-vlogs` (Ellie = Tonia, Jack = Lewis, slowed locally 0.85), each with a free `-slow` copy; videos `video/mei-ice-rink` and `video/noah-bike-park` (Mei = Emilia, Noah = Asher; `.mp4`, `-slow.mp4`, `.vtt`, `-slow.vtt`, `-poster.jpg`, illustrated scenes rendered with Playwright, green palette, brand "English Lab · 2 ESO"). The real exam recording (Maya = Charlotte, Ben = Leo Moreno, normal only) is only in project files `ls2-2eso/exam/audio/`. Credits used for this LS: about 6,585.
- **Exam:** same format as 4ESO LS2 (Roger's *Exam A* model, 43 questions, /100, writing = the vlog script, 5.1 level NA 8 · AS 15 · AN 23 · AE 30). Word exam + teacher's key in project files `ls2-2eso/exam/` (`exam-data.js`, `make-docx.js`), never in the repo. Sample exam on the site: `sample-exam.html` (*Two vlogs: Ellie and Jack*).
- **Vocabulary and grammar summary:** student PDFs per route in `downloads/ls2-vocabulary-grammar-{insecure,safe,daring}.pdf` (from the board; Insecure in Catalan), teacher Word in project files `ls2-2eso/grammar-vocabulary/`; built from the data files by `build/dump.js` + `build/build.py`, converted with LibreOffice and stamped with `tools/stamp-copyright.py`.
- **Catalogue and plans:** `EL_LS` + 13 entries with ids `2eso/ls2-…` in `el-catalog.js` (steps: LP2 `log`, LP3 `chart`, LP4 `factfile`, final product `prep`, `source`, `slides`, `script`, `edit`, `feedback`, `tips`); `EL_GRADING["2eso-free-time-live"]`; `EL_PLAN["2eso-free-time-live"]` (2ESO-A and 2ESO-B, exam Fri 20 Nov 2026 for both, vlog due Thu 10 Dec, Reflect Fri 11 Dec). Criteria texts come from `docs/2eso/programme.md` through `node tools/gen-criteria.js .` (the generator now parses both programmes).
- **Effective-learning rules** as in 4ESO LS2: recall warm-up without notes on every Learning Path, earlier words back in the flashcards and recall, 80 % to complete, typed answers shown after the second wrong try, every Learning Path ends with a product piece, checkpoints between the Learning Paths they review, first try counts (`countsNote`).

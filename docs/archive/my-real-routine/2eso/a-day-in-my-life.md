## Objectives and prerequisites

**Documented student-facing purpose (exact source wording):**

> Learn useful routine expressions, practise them, then use them in your own writing and speaking.

**Route notes (exact source wording):**

| Insecure | Safe | Daring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comença amb expressions molt freqüents i frases curtes. | Practise more expressions and combine them with growing independence. | Use studied collocations precisely and connect your ideas independently. |

The page provides no separate measurable objectives or prerequisite checklist. The LS orders **Getting started** before this page; that warm-up explicitly says **“This session is only a warm-up. It does not introduce new vocabulary.”** No completion gate enforces that sequence. Knowledge of I/he/she, basic times and routine actions is used in the activities but is not documented as an agreed prerequisite threshold. Whether to formalize these prerequisites is **unresolved**; no new prerequisite is imposed here.

### Vocabulary scaffolding and self-report

- Explore instruction and Study cards instruction are in the route-aware inventory above.
- Tooltip support is exposed on hover and focus. A flashcard presents category + expression on the front and the Catalan meaning + the same English example on the back. Its fixed back label is **“Català + example”**.
- The summary is generated as **“[N] expressions. Les tornaràs a veure a les targetes i a les activitats.”** in **every route**, with N = 31 / 60 / 101.
- Card counter: **“Card [i] of [N] · ✓ Known”** or **“Card [i] of [N] · Practising”**, shared English wording.
- Clicking **“I know it ✓”** marks that card known. All cards known completes `cards`; clicking **“Ja les he explorat ✓”** completes `explore`. Neither checks recall objectively.

## Grammar and worked examples actually present

There is **no standalone grammar explanation panel** in this session. The activities use Present Simple, frequency adverbs, time expressions, ordering and connectors. Third-person forms occur in the reading inputs, but a new explanation of third-person rules is not supplied here. The LS's later Present Simple page remains a separate session. Do not invent a grammar mini-lesson for this draft.

The Build instruction explicitly scaffolds **subject → verb → details** and includes **“I have breakfast before school.”** The exact route instruction is in the inventory. The following are all Build items; each token bank is shuffled, with selected tokens removable. The displayed answer must match the complete model string; no alternative order is accepted by the handler.

## Match — all curated rounds and acceptance rules

The route-aware Match instruction explicitly restricts answers to studied Learning Path expressions and says identical tiles are interchangeable; its exact wording is in the inventory.

- Insecure uses its **4 rounds of 8 pairs**; Safe uses its **4 rounds of 8 pairs**; Daring uses its **8 rounds of 8 pairs**. Safe and Daring's displayed rounds focus on their additions, although their vocabulary decks are cumulative.
- Left and right tiles are shuffled independently. A new mix advances cyclically to the next curated round. Re-rendering after a route switch also advances the selected route's round index.
- The handler joins the chosen left/right labels, lowercases, collapses whitespace and trims. It accepts a label combination from the cumulative taught matching records, not the original tile ID/position. Duplicate tiles on either side are interchangeable.
- The **Studied source entry** column below is the exact vocabulary record used to verify that a pair is taught. The **accepted displayed pair** is left + space + right; it need not equal that source entry when the entry represents alternatives.
- Slash-containing tile labels are literal source labels. The handler does not split them into natural-language alternatives or accept arbitrary untaught combinations.
- Each round is checked for taught records and competing studied pair combinations. All 16 curated rounds pass these source checks. That is not certification that every example or expression is pedagogically appropriate.

Every placeholder is determined by the vocabulary/item tables above. The score is **“[score] / [items in current set] correct”**. Metadata is **“Set [s] of [total]”** and **“[reached] / [deck size] expressions reached”**. Checking a set marks it seen **regardless of accuracy**. Checking all sets completes `practice`; there is no accuracy threshold. **“Next set →”** cycles through all sets. Single-word adverb items show no remaining expression and are therefore particularly weak tasks; retain them in this extract and review them.

## Scoring and route-specific completion

The same completion rules apply to all routes; content and deck sizes differ. Progress is stored separately by route on this device. It counts six phases:

| Phase | Actual condition for completion | What is not checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vocabulary | `explore` and all `cards` marked known | Meaning recall / pronunciation |
| Practice | `match` (one round), `practice` (every set checked) and `build` (4/4) | All matching rounds; Practise accuracy |
| Listening | At least 5/6 correct | Explanation of evidence; retention |
| Reading | At least 5/6 correct | Written justification; critical source evaluation |
| Writing | Non-empty text sent to AI feedback | Word range, expression count, connectors, revision |
| Speaking | A recording is stopped and WAV is produced | Length, quality, sentence/expression count, reviewed transcript or revision |

Scored closed tasks award one point per correct item. Wrong or missing responses receive zero; retries have no score penalty. Marked completion is persistent: later mistakes or empty/reset writing do not revoke it. The initial HTML says **“0 / 5”**, but `updateProgress` renders **“[n] / 6”**; the runtime has six phases. No overall numeric grade, weighting or competency attainment rubric is implemented in this pilot.

The LS's assessment weights concern the Final Product and are recorded in the overview. They must not be silently applied to this session's completion flags.

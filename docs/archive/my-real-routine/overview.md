## Final Product

**Previously documented requirements — preserved unchanged.**

- **2ESO:** Insecure interviews and describes a classmate; Safe does so in more detail; **only Daring** also compares with the student’s own routine, including similarities and differences.
- **4ESO:** Insecure interviews and describes the classmate only; **Safe and Daring** also compare with the student’s own routine. Daring develops a richer, more independent comparison with evidence and interpretation.
- Highlight comparison as a core requirement wherever it applies; never add it to routes that do not require it.
- **Every route includes a healthy-habits reflection:** Insecure gives a guided judgment and a simple reason; Safe justifies it with specific habits; Daring develops the reasoning and suggests respectful, realistic improvements where appropriate. Comparison routes reflect on both routines.
- Workflow: **Interview → Notes → Google Slides → Prepare → Record presentation → Review → Submit**.
- The final submission is a video presenting **Google Slides with the student’s face/camera and slides visible together in picture-in-picture**, with clear voice. State this explicitly; do not substitute vague recording-tool instructions or audio-only submission.
- Keep WAV/audio recording for short Speaking practice, not the main Final Product.
- Insecure retains Catalan guidance for every step and a worked chain: **interview answer → notes → slide content → spoken sentence → healthy-habit reflection**, with English target-language examples.

## Additional Final Product details — extracted draft

The website supplements the preserved requirements with the following route-specific tasks and recording guidance. These extracts do not authorize extra requirements.

### 2ESO / Insecure

**Task (exact source wording)**

> Prepara 4–6 idees curtes sobre la rutina del company i una reflexió senzilla sobre si és saludable. Canvia I per he/she i revisa wakes, has, goes i does. No comparis la seva rutina amb la teva.

**Recording guidance (exact source wording)**

- N’hi ha prou amb uns 45–60 segons.
- Explica 4–6 idees clares.
- Acaba amb una reflexió: I think his/her routine is… because…
- Parla a poc a poc i utilitza les diapositives com a ajuda, sense llegir-ho tot.
- Al vídeo s’han de veure alhora la teva cara i les diapositives, i s’ha de sentir la teva veu.

**Assessment guidance (exact source wording)**

> Descriu el company amb frases simples. La reflexió ha de dir què et sembla la rutina i per què, amb un detall real. En aquesta ruta no es demana comparació.

### 2ESO / Safe

**Task (exact source wording)**

> Describe your classmate’s routine in detail. Organise it clearly, use target vocabulary and connectors, and finish with a justified health reflection. Compare parts of that routine if useful; comparison with your own routine is not required.

**Recording guidance (exact source wording)**

- Aim for about 1–1½ minutes.
- Sequence your classmate’s routine clearly and use several studied expressions.
- Include because and but or although.
- Explain whether the routine seems healthy, using details from the interview.
- Keep your face and slides visible together and use the slides as support.

**Assessment guidance (exact source wording)**

> A detailed, organised description and a justified health reflection are required. Comparison with your own routine is reserved for Daring.

### 2ESO / Daring

**Task (exact source wording)**

> Present your classmate’s routine and compare it with your own. Include similarities and differences. Reflect on whether both routines are healthy, justify your views with evidence from each routine, and suggest a realistic improvement where appropriate.

**Recording guidance (exact source wording)**

- Aim for about 1½–2 minutes.
- Describe and compare both routines: similarities and differences.
- Use richer connectors such as whereas, although or however and natural studied expressions.
- Give a health judgment about each routine, with evidence from both.
- Suggest a realistic improvement if the evidence shows a habit could be healthier.
- Keep your face and slides visible together; speak independently rather than reading.

**Assessment guidance (exact source wording)**

> Comparison is the extra challenge for Daring. Support your health judgments about both routines with evidence and consider a realistic improvement.

### 4ESO / Insecure

**Task (exact source wording)**

> Prepara una descripció curta de la rutina del company i una reflexió senzilla sobre si els seus hàbits són saludables. Canvia I per he/she i revisa wakes, has, goes i does. No comparis la seva rutina amb la teva.

**Recording guidance (exact source wording)**

- Explica el dia en un ordre clar.
- Utilitza expressions de rutina treballades.
- Acaba amb una reflexió: I think his/her routine is… because…
- Parla a poc a poc i utilitza les diapositives com a ajuda, sense llegir-ho tot.
- Al vídeo s’han de veure alhora la teva cara i les diapositives, i s’ha de sentir la teva veu.

**Assessment guidance (exact source wording)**

> Descriu el company amb frases simples. La reflexió ha de dir què et sembla la rutina i per què, amb un detall real. En aquesta ruta no es demana comparació.

### 4ESO / Safe

**Task (exact source wording)**

> Describe your classmate’s routine and compare it with your own. Include similarities and differences, causes and consequences. Judge whether both routines are healthy and justify your reflection using details from each routine.

**Recording guidance (exact source wording)**

- Present both routines in a clear order.
- Explain similarities and differences using cause, contrast and consequence.
- Use natural studied expressions.
- Judge the healthiness of both routines and justify your views with details from each.
- Keep your face and slides visible together and your voice clear.

**Assessment guidance (exact source wording)**

> A clear comparison and a health reflection justified with details from both routines are required; use accurate language and relevant connectors.

### 4ESO / Daring

**Task (exact source wording)**

> Develop an evidence-based description and comparison of both routines. Reflect critically on healthy and unhealthy habits, explain what the evidence suggests about each routine and propose respectful, realistic improvements where appropriate.

**Recording guidance (exact source wording)**

- Develop both the description and the comparison rather than listing isolated habits.
- Use richer studied expressions and connectors.
- Distinguish reported evidence from your interpretation.
- Judge healthy and unhealthy habits in both routines and explain your reasoning.
- Suggest realistic, respectful improvements when the evidence warrants them.
- Keep your face and slides visible together and present independently.

**Assessment guidance (exact source wording)**

> Develop your comparison and critical health reflection using evidence from both routines. Explain interpretations and suggest appropriate, realistic improvements.

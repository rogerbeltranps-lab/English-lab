# 2ESO · My Real Routine

**Draft for review.** Existing Final Product requirements are retained; website extracts are not automatically approved. [Shared LS guidance](../my-real-routine.md).

## 1. Learning objectives

- Describe your own routine and a classmate’s routine clearly.
- Use everyday expressions, Present Simple and do/does questions.
- Understand routine descriptions through listening and reading.
- Prepare short written and spoken descriptions with useful connectors.
- Interview a classmate, present their routine and justify a healthy-habits reflection; Daring also compares both routines.

## 3. Final Product

Interview → Notes → Google Slides → Prepare → Record presentation → Review → Submit.

Submit a **video with Google Slides, your face visible in picture-in-picture and a clear voice**. WAV/audio is for short Speaking practice, not the Final Product.

| Route | Main task | Healthy-habits reflection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Insecure** | Interview and describe a classmate in short, supported sentences. **No comparison with your own routine.** | Guided judgment and a simple reason using a real interview detail. |
| **Safe** | Describe the classmate in more detail and a clear sequence. **No required comparison with your own routine.** | Justify the judgment with specific habits. |
| **Daring** | Describe the classmate and **compare both routines: similarities and differences**. | Reflect on both routines with evidence; develop the reasoning and suggest respectful, realistic improvements where appropriate. |

Insecure retains Catalan guidance at every step, with English examples linking **interview answer → notes → slide content → spoken sentence → healthy-habit reflection**. Discuss habits, not bodies or weight.

## 5. Grammar

### Insecure

- **Present Simple per a hàbits:** *I wake up at seven.*
- **Afirmativa:** I/you/we/they + verb base; he/she/it + -s/-es; *watch → watches, study → studies, have → has, do → does, go → goes*.
- **Negativa:** don’t/doesn’t + verb base: *She doesn’t get up late.*
- **Preguntes i respostes curtes:** *Do you have breakfast? — Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.*; *Does she go to school on foot? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.*; *When does she get home?*
- **Freqüència:** always, usually, often, sometimes, never abans del verb principal i després de be: *She usually has breakfast. She is usually tired.* Never ja és negatiu.
- **Ordre i raó:** Then, After that, because. Pronunciació de la tercera persona: *gets /s/, goes /z/, watches /ɪz/*.

### Safe

Retains the same Present Simple system, with less scaffolding:

- Agreement in coordinated verbs: *She gets home and does her homework.*
- Negatives, questions and short answers: *She doesn’t go out on weekdays. Does she go out on weekdays?*
- Expression patterns: **at + time; for + place; with + thing; by + verb-ing; on + day**.
- Sequence, reasons and contrast: **Then, After that, because, but, although, also**.

### Daring

Retains Insecure and Safe forms; applies them independently in longer, connected descriptions:

- Maintain agreement and the base verb after does/doesn’t across clauses: *Although Mia usually gets home early, she doesn’t always start her homework immediately.*
- Frequency questions: **How often + does + subject + base verb?**
- Compare and connect: **both; earlier than / later than; while; whereas; although; however; because; so; as a result**.

# 4ESO · My Real Routine

**Draft for review.** Existing Final Product requirements are retained; website extracts are not automatically approved. [Shared LS guidance](../my-real-routine.md).

## 1. Learning objectives

- Describe routines, commuting and daily responsibilities in natural English.
- Use Present Simple, phrasal verbs and idiomatic expressions in context.
- Understand realistic spoken and written routines and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Connect ideas through cause, contrast and consequence.
- Present a classmate’s routine and justify a health reflection; Safe and Daring compare both routines, with greater depth and independence in Daring.

## 5. Grammar

### Insecure

- **Present Simple per a hàbits:** *I study every afternoon.*
- **Afirmativa:** I/you/we/they + verb base; he/she/it + -s/-es: *She works. He watches TV.*; *have → has, do → does, go → goes*.
- **Negativa:** don’t/doesn’t + verb base: *She doesn’t study at night.*
- **Preguntes i respostes curtes:** *Do you walk to school? — Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.*; *Does she have breakfast? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.*
- **Freqüència:** always, usually, often, sometimes, never abans del verb principal i després de be: *She usually studies after school. She is often tired.* Never ja és negatiu.
- **Seqüència i connexió:** First, Then, After that, Finally; because, also, but.
- La pàgina de gramàtica treballa només Present Simple, sense patrons de phrasal verbs. **Useful expressions** també contrasta una rutina amb un fet acabat: *Alex usually gets up at seven, but yesterday he slept in.*

### Safe

Retains the Present Simple system and adds:

- Consistent subject–verb agreement, negative/question forms and frequency placement: *She gets home and does her homework.*
- Phrasal verbs as **verb + particle**, distinguishing uses with and without an object: *I pick up the kids* / *I wind down* / *I turn in*.
- **Separable, noun object:** *I pick up the kids. = I pick the kids up.*; *I put off my homework. = I put my homework off.*
- **Separable, pronoun object:** *I pick them up. I put it off.* The pronoun goes between verb and particle.
- **Fixed three-part expressions:** *I catch up on a show. → I catch up on it.*; *I get down to my homework. → I get down to it.*
- Expression patterns: **at + time; for + place; with + thing; by + verb-ing; on + day**.
- Reasons, comparison and consequence: **because, but, although, whereas, however, while, both, so**. Present Simple for usual routines; Past Simple for a specified finished event.

### Daring

Retains Safe grammar and develops independent, precise use:

- Explain object position by the expression's pattern: *I put it off* versus *I get down to it*.
- Maintain Present Simple agreement across longer descriptions; distinguish habitual actions from finished events.
- Sequence, cause, contrast and consequence: **First, Then, After that, Finally; because; although, whereas, however, while; so, as a result**.
- **After + verb-ing:** *After whipping up dinner, I get down to work.*
- Compare shared habits with **both** and time differences with **earlier / later**; distinguish reported evidence from interpretation.

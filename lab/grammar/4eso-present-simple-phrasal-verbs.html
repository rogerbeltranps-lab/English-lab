1 · What is a phrasal verb?

Add a particle, get a new meaning

What does it mean? Guess first, then check.

verb + particle

A particle is a small word like up, down, in, out, off. The verb and the particle work together as one verb. Learn them together, like one word.

Un phrasal verb és un verb + una paraula petita (partícula). Junts tenen un significat nou: turn in = anar a dormir, eat out = menjar fora, wind down = relaxar-se.

3 · What is an object?

The object gets the action

Iwho does it?
pick upthe action
my brotherwho gets the action?
= the object

Normal verbs have objects too

I eat an apple. · I watch TV. · I call my friend.

Eat WHAT? → an apple. Call WHO? → my friend.

L’objecte (complement directe) és la persona o la cosa que rep l’acció. Per trobar-lo pregunta WHAT? (què?) o WHO? (a qui?). Quan o on (at ten, on Fridays, at home) no són objectes.

An object can be…

a person: my brother, my sister

a thing: my homework, a snack, messages

a pronoun: it, them, him, her

These are NOT objects

WHEN? at ten · on Fridays · after school

WHERE? at home · to school

I turn in at ten. → no object

3 · Find the object

Does something come after it?

Ask WHAT? or WHO?

I pick up… WHO? → my brother. That is the object.

I turn in… WHAT? → nothing. No object.

at ten answers WHEN?, so it is not an object.

L’objecte és la persona o cosa que rep l’acció. Pregunta WHAT? (què?) o WHO? (qui?). Si no hi ha resposta, no hi ha objecte.

Find the object. No object? Choose No object.

4 · The big picture

Our phrasal verbs fit in three groups

1 No object

Nothing after it

get up · head out · set off · wind down · turn in · sleep in · go out · stay in · eat out

I turn in at ten.

2 Can split

Object in two places

pick up · put off · whip up · wake up (someone)

I pick them up.

3 Three words

Never split

catch up on · get down to

I get down to it.

Grup 1: no porten objecte. Grup 2: es poden separar (l’objecte pot anar al mig). Grup 3: tres paraules, no es separen mai.

1 No object

Nothing after the phrasal verb

I get up at seven.
We go out on Fridays.
I sleep in on Sundays.

After these verbs we can say when or where, but there is no object.

Aquests verbs no porten objecte. Després pots dir quan (at seven) o on, però no una persona o cosa.

Some can add an extra word (optional)

I head out.
I head out for school.

I set off.
I set off for school.

I wind down.
I wind down with music.

for + place and with + thing are optional: both sentences are correct.

Add extra information

Common prepositions add more information

at+ time

I turn in at ten.

on+ day

I sleep in on Sundays.

by+ verb-ing

I wind down by listening to music.

This part is extra: it says when or how. You can take the whole part away (at ten) and the sentence is still correct.

I turn in. ✓ · I turn in at ten. ✓

You can use these with any phrasal verb. Next slide: some verbs also have their own preposition.

Després d’un phrasal verb podem afegir informació amb les preposicions de sempre: at + hora, on + dia, by + verb-ing. És opcional: es pot treure tota la part (at ten).

1 · The verb’s own preposition: learn it by heart

Some phrasal verbs come with their own preposition. There is no rule: learn the verb and its preposition together, as one chunk.

Always needed:

catch up on + thingget down to + thing

Optional, but when you add a place or a thing, use this one:

head out for + placeset off for + placewind down with + thing

2 · Extra information: any phrasal verb

All phrasal verbs, also the ones in box 1, can add more information with the common prepositions: at + time · on + day · by + verb-ing.

I turn in at ten. · I turn in on Sundays.

I catch up on messages on the bus.

I head out for school at eight.

First the verb’s own preposition (box 1), then the extra information (box 2).

1) Alguns phrasal verbs tenen la seva pròpia preposició: cal aprendre-la de memòria, com un bloc (catch up on, get down to, head out for…). 2) Tots els phrasal verbs, també aquests, poden afegir informació amb la preposició que calgui (at + hora, on + dia…): I catch up on messages on the bus.

2 Can split

The object can go in two places

With a noun: two places ✓

I pick up my brother. ✓
I pick my brother up. ✓

With it / them / him / her: only in the middle

I pick him up. ✓
I pick up him. ✗

Amb un nom, l’objecte pot anar després o al mig. Amb un pronom (it, them, him, her), sempre al mig: pick him up.

Change the object to it / him / her.

3 Three words

Three words that never split

catch up on · get down to

verb + particle + preposition. The object always goes at the end, noun or pronoun.

I catch up on emails. → I catch up on them.

I get down to my homework. → I get down to it.

on and to are always needed here.

Tres paraules: verb + partícula + preposició. No es separen mai: l’objecte va sempre al final. I get down to it, no I get it down to.

2 Can split

Pronouns always go in the middle

With it, them, him, her, the phrasal verb always splits:

verb + pronoun + particle

I pick him up. ✓

I pick up him. ✗

Which pronoun?

my brother → him · my sister → her
my homework, a snack → it · the kids, messages → them

3 Three words

Careful: three-word verbs never split, not even with a pronoun. The pronoun goes at the end: I get down to it.

Amb un pronom (it, them, him, her), els phrasal verbs del grup 2 sempre se separen: pick him up, mai pick up him. Els de tres paraules no se separen mai: get down to it.

6 · He / she / it

She picks him up

With he / she / it, add -s to the verb, not to the particle.

She picks him up. ✓ She pick him ups.

He catches up on messages.

Negative: doesn’t + verb without -s

She doesn’t wind down.

Question: Does + he / she + verb without -s

Does he get down to it?

Amb he / she / it el verb porta -s (she picks up). A la negativa i a la pregunta, la -s va a does / doesn’t i el verb no en porta: She doesn’t pick him up. Does she pick him up?

The verb to be

am, is, are

+−?
Iam · I’mI’m notAm I…?
he / she / itis · she’sisn’tIs she…?
you / we / theyare · they’rearen’tAre you…?

No do / does with be. In questions, be jumps in front of the subject: Is she tired?

Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, I’m not. · Yes, she is. / No, she isn’t. Don’t use the short form in a yes answer: Yes, I’m.

Age uses be: I’m twelve. not I have twelve years.

To be = ser / estar. Serveix per descriure: tired (cansat), at school (a l’escola), twelve (dotze anys). No porta do / does: Is she tired?, no Does she is tired? Edat: I’m twelve (no I have twelve years).

Rule 1 · Affirmative

Who does the action?

I / you / we / they+base verb

I get up at seven.

he / she / it+verb-s

She gets up at seven.

Amb I / you / we / they, utilitza la forma base: I get up. Amb he / she / it, afegeix normalment -s: She gets up.

Rule 2 · Negative

don’t and doesn’t

I / you / we / they+don’t+base verb
he / she / it+doesn’t+base verb

The -s moves to doesn’t. The main verb has no -s.

She doesn’t gets up late.

✗

Amb doesn’t, la marca de tercera persona ja és a l’auxiliar: el verb principal no porta -s.

Rule 3 · Questions and short answers

Do you…? Does she…?

Do+I / you / we / they+base verb…?
Does+he / she / it+base verb…?

Short answers repeat the helper, not the main verb: Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.

Does she goes to school?

✗

Amb does, fes servir go, no goes. Resposta curta: Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. · Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.

be or do / does?

Is she…? or Does she…?

am / is / are→ how someone is, where, age

tired · hungry · at school · late · twelve

do / does→ actions

get up · have breakfast · go · watch · study

Never both together.

She doesn’t be tired.

She isn’t tired.

✗

Does she is at school?

Is she at school?

✗

Is Leo get up early?

Does Leo get up early?

✗

Si la frase té am / is / are, no hi posis do / does. Si té un verb d’acció, necessites do / does per a la negativa i la pregunta.

Rule 3b · Negative questions

Don’t you…? Doesn’t she…?

Don’t+I / you / we / they+base verb…?
Doesn’t+he / she / it+base verb…?
Isn’t she…? · Aren’t you…? · Aren’t I…?

Always the short form at the start: Doesn’t she…?

When do we use them?

Surprise: Don’t you like pizza? I love it!

Checking what you think is true: Doesn’t Mia get up at seven?

Suggestion: Why don’t you go to bed earlier?

En anglès la resposta depèn dels fets, no de la pregunta. Don’t you have breakfast? — Yes, I do = sí que esmorzo. No, I don’t = no esmorzo. Compte: no diguis Yes, I don’t.

Answer the facts. What does the answer mean?

Yes = it happens. No = it doesn’t happen. The answers are the same as for normal questions.

Wh- questions

Where, when, who, how…

Question word+do / does+subject+base verb…?

Where does Leo have lunch?

Question word+am / is / are+subject…?

Why is Leo tired?

Remember the order with QASI: Question word · Auxiliary · Subject · Infinitive (base verb).

Ordre de la pregunta: paraula interrogativa + do / does + subjecte + verb. No diguis Where Leo has lunch? sinó Where does Leo have lunch?

Wh- questions · practice

Ask about the dashed part

Who has two patterns

About the subject (the person who does it): no do / does, and the verb keeps the -s.

Who gets up first?

Mia does.

About another person: do / does + subject, like other questions.

Who does Leo play with?

With Mia.

Who subjecte: Who gets up first? (Qui s’aixeca primer?) sense does. Who complement: Who does Leo play with? (Amb qui juga en Leo?) amb does i la preposició al final.

Rule 4 · Frequency words

always, usually, often, sometimes, never

Sheusuallyhas→ before the main verb
Sheisusually→ after be

never is already negative: He never sleeps in, not He doesn’t never sleep in.

Van normalment abans del verb principal: She usually has breakfast. Amb be, van després: She is usually tired. Never ja expressa una idea negativa: no cal afegir don’t / doesn’t.

Watch out

do can be the action or the helper

In do your homework, do is the action. In questions and negatives, we also need do / does as a helper. Sometimes both appear together.

She does her homework.

Does she do her homework?

She doesn’t do her homework.

1 · Seasons

Which season is it?

✋ 1 = spring · 2 = summer · 3 = autumn · 4 = winter

🌸 springMarch · April · May

☀️ summerJune · July · August

🍂 autumnSept · Oct · Nov · in the USA: fall

❄️ winterDecember · January · February

Remember: in + season → in autumn, in winter.

2 · Weather

What’s the weather like?

✋ Show 1, 2 or 3 fingers
💬 Turn and talk

sunny, cloudy, windy, rainy, foggy: what do they have in common?

noun + -y = what the weather is like

sun → sunnycloud → cloudywind → windyrain → rainyfog → foggy

sun and fog double the last letter: sunny, foggy. Ask: What’s the weather like? → It’s sunny.

English Lab · ESO

Make your English real.

An app to teach and learn English in secondary school. It follows the Spanish and Catalan curriculum and is designed with a clear method: learning situations, competences and a structured plan of grammar and vocabulary, with every student working at their own level.

Hi, I'm Roger

I’m a curious mind, always learning something new and looking for better ways to teach. I built English Lab so my students learn in a meaningful way, each at their own level, and I make sure they work on all four communicative skills equally: listening, reading, speaking and writing.

What's inside

📚

Learning situations and competences

Each unit is a learning situation with a real final product. Listening, reading, speaking and writing are worked equally, and marks are given by competence and criterion, as the curriculum asks.

🧩

Structured grammar and vocabulary

Grammar and vocabulary come in a clear order, level by level, from A1 to B1+, with a study guide for every learning path and summaries to download.

🎧

Shadowing, flashcards and games

Shadowing videos for pronunciation and fluency, flashcards for vocabulary, and grammar theory that turns into class games played together.

🎯

Every student at their level

Four levels (Starter, Base, Core and Plus) with the same goals, so every student works on the same topic at the level that fits them.

✅

Corrected straight away

Activities are corrected automatically. Students see their answers and corrections at once, and they stay there when they come back.

📖

Stories to read in chapters

A serial story for each course, one chapter per learning path and written in four levels, to keep reading at home.

🏠

Each student's own board

Every student has a board with what to do next, their mark so far, the teacher's feedback and a self-assessment at the end of each unit.

🗓

Homework with reminders

The teacher sets homework from the dashboard. Students find it on their board and get a summary of the week by email.

📊

The teacher follows every learning path

The teacher sees what each student has done, what is missing and how they are doing in each competence, and sends announcements and marks from one dashboard.

🔒

Focused in class

In class the app goes full screen: the teacher sees who is in the app and who has left it, and can take the whole group to the same activity, so students stay on task.

📝

Exams in exam mode

Exams are done in the app in exam mode: they open in full screen, and if a student leaves it, the exam is handed in.

💻

Chromebooks and phones

Made for school Chromebooks, and it works on phones too, where students can add it to the home screen like an app.

📚 Study

Grammar & discourse

  • Present Continuous: am / is / are + -ing, affirmative, negative, questions and short answers
  • -ing spelling: dance → dancing, swim → swimming, play → playing
  • Time markers: now, right now, at the moment, today, this weekend; usually, on Saturday mornings
  • like / love / hate + -ing (review) and imperatives for screen tips (review)
  • Safe: Present Simple vs Present Continuous; state verbs with no continuous (want, need, know, prefer…)
  • Daring: Present Continuous for temporary situations (This month I’m training…); think with two meanings; longer descriptions with and, but, because, so

Vocabulary & expressions

  • Free-time activities: play football / the guitar, go swimming / skating, do karate, draw, read comics, meet friends…
  • Clothes and kit: T-shirt, trainers, tracksuit, shorts, cap, helmet, wear a uniform / a costume
  • Weather: It’s sunny / cloudy / windy / cold / hot; It’s raining / snowing
  • Technology: watch videos, play online, chat with friends, take photos, record a video, send a message
  • Safe: events (concert, match, festival, tournament, win / lose, score a goal), foggy, stormy, freezing, degrees; upload a video, follow a channel, screen time, social media; take a break, switch off your phone
  • Daring: be good at, spend time + -ing, join a club, train hard; state verbs; turn off notifications, set a time limit, keep your phone out of your bedroom
  • Words you will hear or read: livestream, subscribers, highlights, half-time, fans, crowd

What will you learn?

Learning objectives of this Learning Situation:

  • O1 · Interpret short vlogs, posts and web pages about hobbies and events, using images, key words and context, in order to understand what is happening and what people usually do. (CE4 · 4.1 + CE2 · 2.2)
  • O2 · Evaluate whether online sources about the chosen hobby or event are reliable and suitable, and organise the information in a fact file that credits them, in order to use accurate content in the vlog. (CE4 · 4.2 + CE6 · 6.2)
  • O3 · Apply the Present Continuous and its contrast with the Present Simple, planning, recording and revising the vlog, in order to report live what people are doing compared with what they usually do. (CE3 · 3.2 + CD2 · 2.2)
  • O4 · Create a vlog script and slides with time expressions, likes and dislikes and screen-use tips with imperatives, in order to present a hobby or an event and promote a healthy use of screens. (CE5 · 5.1 + CD4 · 4.5)

How will we work?

Read & notice → Practise words → Listen & compare → Check the source → Write & record → Reflect.

  • Remember before you look: every Learning Path opens with a recall quiz without notes.
  • Again and again: words and grammar come back in later Learning Paths and in recall checkpoints.
  • Mix it up: practice mixes new and old language, so you learn to choose the right form.
  • Feedback and a second try: a step is done when you have answered and checked every activity in it.
  • Build your vlog step by step: each Learning Path gives you one piece of your final product, and a vlog buddy helps you with research and feedback.

Curriculum competences

This Learning Situation develops the Catalan curriculum for Foreign Language (Decree 175/2022). The four communication skills are worked in every LS; this one also integrates research and two digital competences.

  • CE4 · Reading: understand posts and web pages about hobbies and events, and judge how reliable a page is.
  • CE2 · Listening: use images, key words and context to understand short vlogs.
  • CE3 · Speaking: plan, record and revise your own vlog.
  • CE5 · Writing: write the vlog script and the slide text following a model.
  • CE6 · Research & information: build a fact file and credit your photos and sources.
  • Digital competence (CD2 and CD4): record and edit a video with guidance, and promote a healthy use of screens.

Assessment criteria

Each assessment criterion of the curriculum, and how this Learning Situation assesses it:

  • 4.1 · Reading: understand and interpret the overall meaning, structure, key information and purpose of simple texts, making the necessary inferences.
    In this LS: Completes a reading log on two posts or web pages about hobbies and events, noting what people are doing, where and when, what they usually do and the purpose of each text (to inform, to invite or to sell). (O1)
  • 4.2 · Reading: assess the form and content of simple texts, judging their quality, reliability and the channel they come from.
    In this LS: Rates two web pages or posts about the chosen hobby or event as reliable or not with at least two reasons from a checklist (author, date, purpose, type of website) and chooses the one they will use. (O2)
  • 2.2 · Listening: select, organise and apply, with guidance, strategies to understand the general meaning, essential information and details of spoken texts, interpreting non-verbal elements.
    In this LS: While watching two short vlogs about hobbies, uses the images, key words and context to complete a listening chart with what each vlogger is doing, what they usually do and two details (place, clothes or weather). (O1)
  • 3.2 · Speaking: apply, with guidance, strategies to plan, produce and revise understandable, coherent texts with suitable physical or digital resources.
    In this LS: Plans, records and revises the “live” vlog in a video with Google Slides and their face in picture-in-picture, contrasting what people are doing now (Present Continuous) with what they usually do (Present Simple), and improves at least one part after watching the first recording. (O3)
  • 5.1 · Writing: organise and write short, understandable texts, with clarity, coherence and cohesion, following set guidelines, about everyday matters close to the student’s experience.
    In this LS: Writes the vlog script and the slide text following a model, with time expressions, likes and dislikes (like / love / hate + -ing) and screen-use tips with imperatives (Beginners: two tips from the model; Insecure: two tips; Safe: three; Daring: three with a reason). (O4)
  • 6.2 · Research: carry out and communicate the results of a research process creatively, organising the information and respecting intellectual property.
    In this LS: Builds a fact file about the hobby or event (Beginners: two facts from one given source; Insecure: four facts from one given source; Safe and Daring: from two sources) and credits the photos and sources on the last slide. (O2)
  • CD2 2.2 · Digital: create and edit digital content with guidance.
    In this LS: Records and edits the vlog with guidance, cutting at least one mistake or long pause, and places each photo with its caption next to its part of the script. (O3)
  • CD4 4.5 · Digital: adopt habits for a healthy use of technology.
    In this LS: Includes screen-use tips that prevent addictive habits (time limits, breaks, no phone before sleeping) and says which one they will apply. (O4)

My Real Routine

Explore your everyday routine, learn useful English and use it to speak and write about real life with confidence.

Your challenge

Explain your routine and a friend’s routine

Use Present Simple, everyday expressions and clear sequencing to describe what you usually do and what another person usually does.

What will you learn?

You will learn how to describe your own routine and another person’s routine naturally and clearly.

  • Present Simple in real routines
  • Do / does questions
  • Daily routine vocabulary
  • Useful phrasal verbs and expressions
  • Short writing and speaking tasks

How will we work?

Short activities, real examples and progressive challenges.

  • Watch and listen to real English
  • Shadow short sentences
  • Build and improve sentences
  • Interview a classmate
  • Write and speak about routines

Grammar

  • Present Simple: affirmative, negative and questions
  • Third person -s
  • Do / does
  • Adverbs of frequency
  • Basic connectors: then, after that, because, but

Vocabulary & expressions

  • Morning and evening routines
  • School, meals, home and free time
  • Times and parts of the day
  • Frequency expressions
  • Phrasal verbs and idiomatic chunks

Grammar

  • Present Simple: affirmative, negative and questions
  • Third person -s
  • Do / does
  • Adverbs of frequency
  • Basic connectors: then, after that, because, but

Vocabulary & expressions

  • Morning and evening routines
  • School, meals, home and free time
  • Times and parts of the day
  • Frequency expressions
  • Phrasal verbs and idiomatic chunks

What will you learn?

You will learn how to describe your own routine and another person’s routine naturally and clearly.

  • Present Simple in real routines
  • Do / does questions
  • Daily routine vocabulary
  • Useful phrasal verbs and expressions
  • Short writing and speaking tasks

How will we work?

Short activities, real examples and progressive challenges.

  • Watch and listen to real English
  • Shadow short sentences
  • Build and improve sentences
  • Interview a classmate
  • Write and speak about routines

Curriculum competences

This Learning Situation develops the Catalan curriculum for Foreign Language through a real communicative challenge.

  • CE3 · Oral communication: speak clearly about everyday routines and interact with a classmate.
  • CE5 · Written & multimodal production: create short, clear Google Slides using the language practised.
  • CPSAA · Personal, social & learning to learn: reflect on everyday habits and wellbeing in a respectful way.
  • Digital competence: create and share a simple multimodal product responsibly.

How the challenge is assessed

The final product is connected directly to these assessment criteria:

  • 3.1: produce a short, structured and understandable oral text about everyday life.
  • 3.2: plan, produce and review the oral presentation with guided strategies and digital resources.
  • 3.3: take part in a short interaction with a classmate respectfully.
  • 5.1: write a short, clear and coherent multimodal text for Google Slides.

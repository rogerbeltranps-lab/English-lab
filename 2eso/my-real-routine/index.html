My Real Routine

En Insecure trobaràs instruccions i ajuda en català: observa els models i practica pas a pas. L’anglès és la llengua dels textos i de la teva producció. Safe redueix l’ajuda i Daring demana més autonomia. Segueix el Learning Path en ordre i prepara les diapositives i el vídeo final amb la guia de Final Product.

Explore your everyday routine, learn useful English and use it to speak and write about real life with confidence.

Your challenge

Present a classmate’s real routine

Interview a classmate, prepare Google Slides and record a video with your face and slides visible together. All routes judge whether the routine is healthy; Daring also compares it with your own.

What will you learn?

You will learn how to describe your own routine and another person’s routine naturally and clearly.

  • Present Simple in real routines
  • Do / does questions
  • Daily routine vocabulary
  • Useful phrasal verbs and expressions
  • Short writing and speaking tasks

How will we work?

Short activities, real examples and progressive challenges.

  • Watch and listen to real English
  • Shadow short sentences
  • Build and improve sentences
  • Interview a classmate
  • Write and speak about routines

Curriculum competences

This Learning Situation develops the Catalan curriculum for Foreign Language through a real communicative challenge.

  • CE3 · Oral communication: speak clearly about everyday routines and interact with a classmate.
  • CE5 · Written & multimodal production: create short, clear Google Slides using the language practised.
  • CPSAA · Personal, social & learning to learn: reflect on everyday habits and wellbeing in a respectful way.
  • Digital competence: create and share a simple multimodal product responsibly.

How the challenge is assessed

The final product is connected directly to these assessment criteria:

  • 3.1: produce a short, structured and understandable oral text about everyday life.
  • 3.2: plan, produce and review the oral presentation with guided strategies and digital resources.
  • 3.3: take part in a short interaction with a classmate respectfully.
  • 5.1: write a short, clear and coherent multimodal text for Google Slides.

Final challenge

From interview to presentation

Use what you have learnt to turn a real classmate interview into a clear digital and oral product.

Insecure

La rutina del meu company o companya

Entrevista un company o companya, pren notes i prepara Google Slides. Grava un vídeo amb la teva cara i les diapositives mentre expliques la seva rutina en anglès i dius si et sembla saludable.

  • 4–6 idees clares amb suport i exemples en català
  • he / she + Present Simple
  • expressions de rutina treballades
  • reflexió guiada: I think his/her routine is… because… amb un detall real

Entrevista → Notes → Google Slides → Prepara → Grava la presentació → Revisa → Lliura. Grava les diapositives amb la teva cara visible en una finestra petita (imatge dins d’imatge) i la teva veu. Revisa el vídeo complet abans de lliurar-lo. Per valorar els hàbits, pregunta’t: aquesta rutina és saludable? Per què sí o per què no? Justifica-ho amb un detall de l’entrevista. Es valora que expliquis la rutina en anglès de manera clara, preparis diapositives útils i revisis el vídeo; no facis comentaris sobre cossos o pes.

Safe

My classmate’s routine

Describe your classmate’s routine in more detail using Google Slides. Record your face and slides together, and justify whether the routine seems healthy. Comparison with your own routine is not required.

  • sequence the routine clearly
  • use several target expressions
  • add a reason or contrast
  • short health reflection justified with details from the routine
Daring

Compare our routines

Describe your classmate’s routine and compare it with your own in a camera-and-slides video. Reflect on whether both routines are healthy, using evidence from each.

  • similarities + differences
  • richer connectors
  • natural collocations
  • health judgments about both routines, justified with evidence
  • a respectful, realistic improvement where appropriate

Interview → Notes → Google Slides → Prepare → Record presentation → Review → Submit. Share the reviewed video where your teacher indicates; the video must show your face and slides together, with clear sound.

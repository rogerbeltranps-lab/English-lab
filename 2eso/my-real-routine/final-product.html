What are you creating?

1 · A real interview

Ask a classmate about their school-day routine and healthy habits.

2 · A Google Site

Organise the most important information with short text and useful visuals.

3 · An oral presentation

Record your screen while you move through the Site and explain the routine.

4 · A short reflection

Notice one positive habit. Daring students also compare it with their own routine.

Insecure route

Interview your classmate

Take short notes. You do not need to write complete sentences while your classmate speaks.

Real interaction

2 Your notes

Build your Google Site

Keep it visual and simple. The Site should support your speaking, not replace it.

Digital product

1 Suggested structure

Home

Title + classmate’s first name or invented name + a simple image or icon.

Morning

Wake up, breakfast, getting ready and going to school.

After school

Getting home, homework, free time, sport or other activities.

Evening

Dinner, screens, winding down and bedtime.

Healthy habits

One or more habits connected to food, water, sleep, movement or screen time.

Compare

A small section showing one similarity or difference between your routines.

2 Keep it clear

Use short sentences, headings, icons or appropriate images. Avoid long paragraphs. Never publish private information about your classmate.

Prepare what you will say

Plan your explanation before you record. Use the Site as a guide, but speak to your audience.

Screen + voice

1 Before you start

Open your Site

Start on the home page and close tabs you do not need.

Check your microphone

Make sure your voice can be heard clearly.

Know your route

Decide which Site sections you will show and in what order.

Speak, do not read

Use your notes only when you need them.

2 While you record

Move through the Site slowly. Give your audience time to see each section while you explain it.

Check & submit

Watch your recording once and check the final product before you hand it in.

Final check

Final checklist

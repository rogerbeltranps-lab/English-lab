Final product

Interview a classmate, prepare Google Slides and record a video with your face and slides visible together. Include a route-appropriate reflection on whether the routine is healthy.

Interview → Notes → Google Slides → Prepare → Record presentation → Review → Submit. Use the slides as visual support, not a script to read.

Your mission

Use the language studied in this Learning Situation to describe a real routine and reflect on its healthiness.

Camera + slides

What are you creating?

1 · Interview & notes

Ask a classmate about their daily routine and collect details for a health reflection.

2 · Google Slides

Organise the routine and health reflection with short English text and useful visuals.

3 · Camera + slides video

Record yourself presenting the slides: your face, slides and voice must be in the same video.

4 · Health reflection

Every route must say whether the routine seems healthy. Support the judgment at the depth required for your route.

5 · Compare

Compare your classmate’s routine with your own. Explain similarities and differences.

1 · Interview your classmate

Interview a classmate. All routes describe the classmate; comparison is added only where your route requires it.

Ask useful questions

Ask follow-up questions when a detail is unclear. Collect evidence about food, water, sleep, movement and screens.

2 · Take notes

Take short notes rather than copying full answers. Turn I into he / she when describing your classmate.

Worked example: from an answer to a reflection

Interview answer → notes → slide sentence → spoken sentence → health reflection:
I have breakfast at seven. → Alex · breakfast 7:00 → He has breakfast at seven. → This is Alex. He has breakfast at seven and drinks water at school. → I think his routine is mostly healthy because he has breakfast and drinks water.
Base your own sentences and judgment on the interview; collect your own evidence too when comparing.

Your interview notes

Write actions, times and relevant habits. If your route compares routines, also note your own details.

3 · Build your Google Slides presentation

Use the slides to support your explanation, not replace it. Follow your route’s description, comparison and health-reflection requirements.

Suggested slide sequence

Title

Presentation title, your first name and your classmate’s first name or an invented name.

Morning

Wake up, breakfast, getting ready and going to school.

After school

Getting home, homework, free time, sport or other activities.

Evening

Dinner, winding down, screen habits and bedtime.

Health reflection

Say whether the routine seems healthy, partly healthy or not very healthy, and explain why with evidence. Evaluate both routines when comparing.

Compare both routines

Include similarities and differences, then support your health judgments with details from both routines.

Check your slides

Use headings, short English sentences, icons or appropriate images. Keep the slides readable; put speaking details in your notes.

4 · Prepare what you will say

Plan and rehearse your explanation in English. Include the health reflection, with comparison only where required.

English presentation model

Build the health reflection

Valoració positiva: I think his/her routine is mostly healthy because he/she…

Valoració amb aspectes a millorar: I think his/her routine is partly healthy because…, but…

Valoració negativa: I think his/her routine is not very healthy because he/she…

Tria només la frase que coincideixi amb les notes. Exemple de detall: drinks water, walks to school o uses screens late at night. No jutgis la persona: parla de l’hàbit.

Your speaking plan

Tip: rehearse once without recording, then present again looking less at your notes.

5 · Record presentation

Record your face and Google Slides together while explaining the presentation in English. Keep the camera visible; a screen-only or audio-only recording is not the final product.

Camera + slides + voice
Open Google Slides

Start the slideshow and close unrelated tabs. Record the slideshow and your camera together, with your face in a small picture-in-picture window over the slides.

Camera + screen + microphone

Select slides/screen, webcam/camera and microphone. Keep your face visible in a camera overlay without covering slide text.

Test first

Make a short test video. Play it back and check that the slides, your face and your voice are all present.

Record and save the video

Present each slide in English, including the health reflection. Stop and save/export the video with camera and slides together.

Final submission: a video with camera + slides + voice. WAV/audio is only for shorter Speaking practice activities in the Learning Path; it does not replace this final video.

6 · Review your presentation

Watch the whole video. Check the routine details, health reflection, camera, slides and sound. Revise the slides or re-record if needed.

Review the full video

7 · Submit the final video

Submit/share the reviewed video presentation, not only slides or an audio file.

Share the recorded presentation

Upload the final video or share its link in the place indicated by your teacher. Check viewing permissions and playback. Share the Google Slides link too if your teacher asks for it; the slides alone do not replace the video.

On pages with that selector, `lp-theme-2eso.css`/`lp-theme-4eso.css` hide the brand number and title; keep the markup, and keep the selector's options complete and ordered (`section-next.js` reads them for "continue to the next Learning Path").
- **Class presentations (grammar and vocabulary decks):** one self-contained page per deck in `lab/grammar/<course>-<topic>.html` (grammar) or `lab/vocabulary/<course>-<topic>.html` (vocabulary), in the course palette, listed in the English Lab Grammar or Vocabulary menu (`lab/index.html`) and linked from its Learning Path page with an `a.deck-link` and `?from=<that page>` (the ← button goes back there). **Vocabulary introductions (Roger, 25 Oct 2026):** the class guesses each new word first (a clue, a picture emoji or a sentence), then the reveal shows the word, a 🔊 button that says it with the browser's English voice (`speechSynthesis`, en-GB; on the projector or on each student's Chromebook) and its Catalan meaning, with a short reason; each section ends with a word wall or the pattern (sun → sunny). **New vocabulary introductions are class games, not slides (Roger, 30 Oct 2026):** the Autumn words slides of Getting Started (4 ESO Seasons & Celebrations) became the game `lab/game/decks/4eso-autumn-words.js` and the slide page and the English Lab Vocabulary menu were removed; see "Class games". **Question-first (Roger, 25 Oct 2026):** lecture decks made him talk too much, so new decks ask the class before they explain: every question shows how students answer (✋ 1–3 fingers, 👍/👎, ✏️ write it, 💬 turn and talk, 🎲 random class-list number, 🏆 team points) and → / Space / a presenter clicker reveals one step at a time (answer, then rule). Reference: `lab/grammar/4eso-present-simple-customs.html` (Roger, 30 Oct 2026: no longer linked from the Learning Path, the LP2 class game replaces it in class; it stays in the English Lab Grammar menu) (`data-step` shows an element, `data-open` reveals its answer, `data-until` removes it, `data-label` names the step on the Next button, `data-ca` holds the Catalan help). Never put student names in a deck: the picker uses class-list numbers. **Every presentation explains the team points (Roger, 25 Oct 2026):** right after the cover it has `<section class="slide" data-el-points></section>` (the reminder: the rules and a quick check, filled by `shared/js/el-deck-points.js`), the 🏆 Teams strip (`#teambar` with `data-course`, `#teamsBtn`) and, before the deck's own script, `shared/js/el-sound.js`, `shared/js/el-points.js` and `shared/js/el-deck-points.js`. **Sound effects (Roger, 25 Oct 2026):** `shared/js/el-sound.js` makes them with the Web Audio API (no sound files): `ELSound.play(name)` for the 3 · 2 · 1 countdown (`tick`, `go`), the random number (`pick`), the timer's end (`time`), + on the team strip (`point`) and the buzzer (`open`, `first`, `right`, `wrong` on the projector; `press`, `win` on students' devices); the deck's 🔊 tool or M mutes them in that browser (`el_sound`). See "Team points" under the teacher dashboard. **Every deck engine keeps the live hooks** (copy them from the reference deck): after each step it dispatches `deckstep` with `info()`; it exposes `window.ELDeck = {info, show, setCa}`; options carry `data-i` (their place before shuffling) and `info().order` sends each slide's order as a string ("2,1,0": Firestore refuses nested arrays); and while `live` is set (`.deck-live` on the root) it hides the nav buttons, tools and rail and ignores the student's keys except C (Catalan) and F (full screen). `#teambar` also has `data-ls` (the LS whose route decides Catalan). See "Buzzer and live view".
- **Class games (Roger, 26 Oct 2026: "like a Kahoot or a Blooket"):** one engine in `lab/game/`, and a game is only content. A new game = a deck file `lab/game/decks/<course>-<topic>.js` that calls `ELGameDeck({title, slides})` plus one line in `lab/game/decks/index.js` (`{id, title, course, emoji, ls:"LS2 · …", lp:"LP1 · …"}`; the board lists the games by course, then LS, then learning path: Roger, 30 Oct 2026). Slides, in order: theory `{theory, img, lines:[…]}` (shown on the board only) and question `{q, img, options:[2–4], why}` where **the first option is the right one** (the board shuffles them, the same order on every reload; `keep:true` keeps the order, e.g. True / False). **Written questions (Roger, 30 Oct 2026):** `{q, img, write:['is cheering', "'s cheering"], why}`: students type the missing words on their device instead of tapping a tile and send them once (`a:{slide: text}` in the same answers doc, no rules change); `write` lists every accepted answer, the first is shown. The board checks them with `ELGame.norm` (lower case, straight apostrophes, single spaces, no final full stop) when it shows the answer, sends the normalised list as `keys[slide]`, and shows the wrong answers without names. The player gets only `q:{text, img, write:true}` before that. A game may alternate tap and written questions (`lab/game/decks/2eso-present-continuous.js`). Text: `**word**` is highlighted, `___` is a gap; `img` is an emoji or an image file. No timer: only right or wrong. **Every game follows these rules (Roger, 26 Oct 2026):** it is at **Safe** level (not easy), and it uses the learning paths' vocabulary in two ways: **passive** (the words appear in the questions and theory texts) and **active** (questions that ask for a word: meaning, use, collocation or preposition). Choose mainly the words that are not common at their level: the [S] lists of the LS overview (`docs/<course>/ls…/overview.md`), not the basic [I] ones. The teacher opens the board (`lab/game/host.html`, also linked from the dashboard's Points box), chooses the game and the group; from then on `host.js` publishes it in `live/{group}` (`game:true`, phase lobby | theory | q | reveal | end, the question and its options but never the right one before it is shown) and every signed-in student of that group is taken from any page into the player (`lab/game/play.html?g=…&from=…`: `el-cloud.js` and `el-deck-points.js` watch `live/{group}`). The player loads no deck file: it shows only the question and four coloured tiles (red ▲, blue ◆, gold ●, green ■), one tap, then green ✓ or red ✗. Answers go to `live/{group}/answers/{email}` and right answers become the same term points as the presentations (`points/{email}`, `teams/{group}.best`; bonus up to +0.5 in proportion to the group's best, `el-points.js`; the dashboard's Points box exports them as CSV). The answer shows by itself once everyone has answered. Music (`ELGame.music`, Web Audio, no files) plays on the board only; students' devices play only their own tap and result sounds. Reference decks: `lab/game/decks/4eso-autumn-words.js`, `lab/game/decks/4eso-celebrations.js`, `lab/game/decks/2eso-present-continuous.js` (tap and written questions alternate; Roger, 30 Oct 2026: the tap-only 4 ESO customs game was deleted as it no longer fits), `lab/game/decks/2eso-free-time.js`. **Theory slides go between the questions** (about 3 theory slides and 15–20 questions), each followed by questions that practise it. Roger's readable guide: `docs/general/class-games-guide.md` (keep it in step with these rules, add each new game to its table, and update the copy in his Drive, Feina > Teacher Studio). **Each game is linked from its learning path for the teacher only** (Roger, 26 Oct 2026): on the page of the learning path whose content it closes, a `<p data-el-teacher hidden>` link to `lab/game/host.html?deck=<id>&from=/English-lab/<that page's path>` (el-cloud.js shows `[data-el-teacher]` only to the teacher's account); it opens the board on that game, ready to choose the group. **Back to the Learning Path (Roger, 30 Oct 2026):** the board shows "← Learning Path" (group choice, game list and end screen) to the `from` page, or to the page that linked to it if there is no `from` (never the dashboard, which has its own button); kept in the tab's sessionStorage (`elGameBack`). The game uses the live and answers documents of the class quiz. Testers answer and get points too (to try it), but their points never set the group's best: the best is taken over the student list only.

## Route language and scaffolding
- **Beginners (Roger, 26 Oct 2026):** a fourth route, A1, below Insecure, for students starting almost from zero. Everything Insecure gets in Catalan, plus heavier scaffolding: a short core word list per LS practised with spaced retrieval (flashcards and recall every session), readings of 60–100 words built from that list at 95–98 % known words with Catalan hover glosses, multiple choice or matching instead of open answers, the slow audio copy with English subtitles from the first viewing, sentence frames with gaps and a word bank, the same final product with fewer requirements, feedback as a Catalan hint and a second try, and a short "how did I do it?" box. Same listenings and videos as everyone, mixed pairs in class (not Beginners with Beginners). Marked on the same NA–AE scale against CEFR A1 can-do statements. **Only the teacher sets it**, like every route (students can ask for it from their board; the teacher accepts or declines). **In code:** `elCa(level)` (`el-catalog.js`) says whether a route reads Catalan; LS2 engines order routes `beginners < insecure < safe < daring`, an untagged item is for every route, and a text, list or rubric with no `beginners` version falls back to `insecure` (`byLevel`, `forLevel`, `final-rubric.js`, the marking PDF, the study PDF). Every LS2 Learning Path page has a Beginners route button (students only see their own). LS1 pages have no Beginners version: `applyLevel` shows them the Insecure one. New LS content adds `beginners` keys where the A1 version differs (readings, questions, tasks, rubric), designed in the LS project. **Seasons & Celebrations (4 ESO) and Free Time, Live! (2 ESO) have Beginners content on every page since 26 Oct 2026** (docs/4eso/ls2-seasons-and-celebrations/overview.md and docs/2eso/ls2-free-time-live/overview.md, section "Beginners route"): the convention is that untagged items are for every route and `r: 'insecure'` marks what starts at Insecure. **The programmes** (docs/4eso/programme.md and docs/2eso/programme.md) carry the Beginners route since 26 Oct 2026 (Roger approved): a Beginners product bullet, core word list and A1 grammar block per LS, and a final chapter "Resum de gramàtica i lèxic · ruta Beginners (A1)" that is the reference for designing Beginners content in any LS.
- **Native (Roger, 30 Oct 2026):** a fifth route above Daring for a native speaker preparing Cambridge C2 Proficiency (4 ESO only for now); students see it as **Pro**. English throughout. **Only the teacher sets it** (`ask:false` in `EL_LEVELS`: students cannot ask for it). Same LS topic and Learning Paths, but C2 Proficiency-style tasks: Reading and Use of English (Part 5 texts, open cloze, word formation, key word transformations, multiple-choice cloze), listening with attitude and implication questions on the class audio, writing to Part 1/Part 2 tasks (article, essay, proposal, letter; 240–320 words; AI feedback on the four Cambridge writing subscales). **Speaking: no recording** (Roger): each speaking step is a `plan` panel (`ls2-engine.js` `R.plan`): the question, a strategy, the student's written plan (first ideas, the best ones and why, the order), then a model plan; handed in as `state.steps[id]`. **In code:** `data/native/<page>.js` sets `window.LP.native = { panelId: panel }`, loaded after the page's data file; `usePanels()` swaps those panels in by id (same ids, so progress, checkpoints, locks and the dashboard work). Getting started (own renderer) reads `window.GS_NATIVE` from `data/native/getting-started.js` (recall, vocab, practice, reading, listening, speaking plan). Anything without a Native version (other panels, LS1, 2 ESO, texts and lists in `byLevel`/`forLevel`) falls back to Daring, including the model page and rubric fallback. **Extra Pro panels:** a `LP.native` entry whose id is not in the page and has `after: '<panel id>'` is inserted after that panel (e.g. the real-video listening: one real native-speed YouTube video per Learning Path on its topic, `realvideo`, with C2 attitude/inference questions written from the video's captions; Roger 30 Oct 2026: real videos only, no generated audio); it is not in `EL_ACTIONS`, so it never changes progress. Getting started shows `GS_NATIVE.video` under the listening. The final product rubric has its own `native` entry (video and email rows on the Cambridge scales, mapped to NA–AE). `el-route-names.js` relabels Native → Pro except in “Native American(s)”; never start a student-facing sentence with a capitalised “Native” otherwise. Question analysis ignores the Native route.
- **Insecure (and Beginners):** pedagogical instructions, explanations, strategy tips, checklists, step-by-step guidance and corrective feedback are in **Catalan**. Use clear scaffolding and worked examples; explain briefly what went wrong and what to notice.
- **Safe:** instructions and explanations are in **English**. Catalan may appear only as limited vocabulary support, such as hover/focus meanings.
- **Daring:** English-first, with greater learner autonomy and less scaffolding.
- **English remains the target language** for vocabulary, listening/reading input, model sentences, interview questions where appropriate, slide content and student production. Do not translate these into Catalan when adding scaffolding.
- Reuse route-aware support such as `shared/js/learning-path-support.js` where appropriate; verify language updates correctly when switching routes.

## Levels, listenings, videos and readings
- Each route has the level agreed in the levels document of the teaching programme:

  | Course | Year horizon | Beginners | Insecure | Safe | Daring |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1ESO | A1 consolidated; starting A2 tasks | Pre-A1 / A1 with a lot of support | A1 with a lot of support | A1 consolidated | A1+ / early A2 |
  | 2ESO | Functional A2 | A1 with a lot of support | A1+ / early A2 with scaffolding | Functional A2 | A2+ / emerging B1 |
  | 3ESO | More autonomous A2; towards B1 | A1 with a lot of support | A2 with support | More autonomous A2 | Emerging B1 |
  | 4ESO | B1 as the final horizon | A1 with a lot of support | A2 / A2+ with support | Indicative B1 | B1 / B1+ |

  Content marked as an extension of the next level (for example B1+ in 4ESO) goes only to Daring. This school year the site covers **2ESO and 4ESO** only.
- **Listenings and videos are pitched at the Safe level**, and every route hears the same input. Do not write separate scripts per route.
- **The activities around that input adapt to each route's level:** questions, tasks, support, hints and the language of instructions. Insecure gets easier questions, support in Catalan and, where possible, a slower version of the audio; Beginners gets the slow copy with subtitles from the first viewing and multiple choice or matching only. Daring gets the more demanding questions (inference, comparison, opinion).
- **Reading texts are different: they vary by route.** Each route reads a version written at its own level (for 4ESO: A2 / A2+ for Insecure, B1 for Safe, B1 / B1+ for Daring), on the same topic and with the same key information, so the whole class can share the content. Adapt length, sentence complexity, vocabulary and grammar to the level; the questions follow the route's text.
- **Per-route readings in code:** in pages built with `ls2-engine.js`, `media.text` is a string or `{beginners, insecure, safe, daring}` (`beginners` optional: without it Beginners reads the Insecure text); standalone pages keep one text per route in a `readingData` / `readingChat` object keyed by level. Questions stay cumulative (`r:`), so every question a route sees must be answerable from that route's text.
- **Scripted videos:** one MP4 at Safe level plus a slower copy for Insecure, stored next to the page in `video/`, with WebVTT subtitles (`.vtt`) that students switch on after a first viewing. The slow copy is made locally with ffmpeg (`atempo=0.85`, longer pauses), never with a second paid generation. Scripts are recorded in the LS docs before production.
- **Shadowing video per LS (Roger, 30–31 Oct 2026; 4 ESO and 2 ESO LS2):** one ~6-minute video per LS at Core level, the same for every route, and **always in this format** (Roger: "always like this"). **Every sentence is marked** (Roger, 31 Oct 2026; `marks` in the spec): stressed syllables underlined `**x**`, some linked words `a‿b` (not every link, one or two per sentence), and ↗ ↘ for the melody (Roger, 31 Oct 2026): ↗ before every comma, ↘ at the end of a statement, ↗ at the end of a yes/no question, ↘ at the end of a wh- question; check against the recording's pitch (praat-parselmouth) and prefer sentences whose recording follows this (avoid ones that clearly fall at a comma or rise at a full stop). A spoken intro (what shadowing is, the 4 steps, tips, with a legend of the marks on the tips slide) and 4 worked examples (stress, linking `==a‿b==`, a question with no question word going up ↗, a who/what/where question going down ↘; Roger, 31 Oct 2026: "explain this in the introduction"), played twice, then "Now you try" (the third and fourth have no narrator line, so the intro audio stays as generated); then about 15 sentences, each: listen and read (subtitled), a chime and "Your turn" pause with a timer bar and the sentence still shown in the same format, listen again with no text, a second pause (pause = 1.25 × speech + 1.5 s, at least 3 s). Sentences are cut from the LS's own recordings, unchanged (no generated audio): `python3 tools/shadowing-video/align-words.py <ls folder> words.json <data files>` gives word times (pocketsphinx), and the cuts are snapped to the quietest 30 ms nearby. Spec per LS in `tools/shadowing-video/<ls>.json`, built with `node tools/shadowing-video/build.js <spec>` into `<ls>/video/shadowing.mp4` plus a poster. The intro voice is Amelia (Emma's voice, ElevenLabs eleven_v3, script in project files `shadowing-video/intro-voice-script.md`): clips `l1`–`l8` in `tools/shadowing-video/voice/intro/`, shared by every LS and course (generated 31 Oct 2026 on Roger's PC with his key in the Windows user variable `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, ~330 credits; the cloud container cannot reach ElevenLabs). **Where it goes (Roger, 31 Oct 2026: keep both):** the Learning Path Shadowing steps stay as they are (short, each path's own listening), and the video is the unit's revision: the first tab of the sample exam (an `info` panel, not a step: nothing saved or counted), the student board next to the summary PDF and the dashboard's Materials tab (`EL_LS[].shadowing`). A new LS adds its video and those three links. The mp4s go to the LS's Drive `Videos` folder like the other videos.
- Mix real, level-appropriate listenings with generated ones. Never call a paid generation service (for example ElevenLabs) or spend its credits without the user's explicit permission for that specific generation. An approved script is not permission to generate.
- **Generated audio files:** MP3s live in the LS folder's `audio/` (for example `4eso/seasons-and-celebrations/audio/culture-bites-origins.mp3`) and are referenced from the LS data as `media.audio`. The slow version for Insecure sits next to it with a `-slow` suffix (`culture-bites-origins-slow.mp3`). Both files are also copied to that LS's `Listenings` folder in Roger's Drive in the same task (see the Drive rules at the top).
- **ElevenLabs settings:** for new audio use the expressive model `eleven_v3` with audio tags in the script (`[excited]`, `[surprised]`, `[laughs]`…), shown to the user before generating; recordings made before 24 Oct 2026 used `eleven_multilingual_v2` without tags. One call per script line (one voice per character), lines joined with 0.45 s pauses and loudness-normalised (`loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5`, mono, 96 kbps). Keep the same voice for a character across an LS; each LS's `AGENTS.md` lists its voices.
- **Choosing voices for new audio:** shortlist 3–4 well-rated ElevenLabs Voice Library voices per character (right accent and age, educational or conversational use case, high usage count) and send the user their free preview clips; generate only after the user picks. Existing recordings keep their voices.
- **Slow versions are always made locally, never with a second paid generation:** apply ffmpeg `atempo=0.85` to the same line clips and join them with 0.9 s pauses.

## The four communicative competences, worked equally (Roger, 30 Oct 2026: “VERY IMPORTANT”)
- **Rule for every LS:** the learning paths work the four communicative competences **equally**: CE2 Listening (comprendre textos orals i multimodals), CE3 Speaking (produir textos orals i participar en interaccions orals), CE4 Reading (comprendre textos escrits i multimodals) and CE5 Writing (produir textos escrits i multimodals). **The exams stay as they are** (“it’s the way we worked”); the sample exam mirrors the exam, so it does not change either.
- **How it is counted** (the dashboard’s whole-year view, “how much it is worked”): the steps of the LS activities per competence from `EL_STEP_CRITERIA` (`el-catalog.js`; a step with two criteria counts half each). **Use of English (vocabulary and grammar) counts as reading and writing alike** (Roger, 30 Oct 2026: students read and then write, complete or choose), so recall, cards, practice, grammar and mixed steps without their own criteria give 4.1 + 5.1. ## Flashcards
- Flashcard practice uses a **random set of up to 10 cards** (all cards if the deck has fewer). Do not make students work through the whole deck.
- The student **flips** the card, then chooses **Got it** or **Again**. The rating buttons stay disabled until the card is flipped. An **Again** card comes back before the student moves on, and the step is complete when every card in the set is **Got it**.
- "New set" gives a different random set. The counter keeps the format `Card 6 of 10 · Practising · 5/10 known`; Insecure shows it and the instructions in Catalan.
- Reuse the existing implementations (`flashcard-set` logic in the vocabulary pages and `shared/js/flashcard-set.js`) instead of writing a new one.

## Answer options
- **Randomize the answer options, never the questions.** Multiple-choice options and dropdown options are shuffled every time a question is shown, so the correct answer is not always in the same position. Question order stays as written.
- Use `learningSupport.shuffled()` / `learningSupport.shuffledIndexes()` from `shared/js/learning-path-support.js` (include that file in every page that renders questions). Keep each option's original value so scoring and feedback do not change.
- Do not change the wording of questions or options when shuffling.

## Assessment and grading
- **Every LS criterion ends with a degree** (NA, AS, AN, AE) that the teacher can justify with its indicators (`indicators.md` of the LS) and a piece of work. Each LS documents its evidence per criterion in a `grading.md`; worked example: [4ESO LS2](docs/4eso/ls2-seasons-and-celebrations/grading.md), also in Roger's reference doc *Worked example: from competence to LS criterion*.
- **Secure recall checkpoints (Roger, 31 Oct 2026: “make sure they don't cheat”; `docs/general/secure-checkpoints.md`):** LS2 checkpoint pages have no answers or hints in their data (`secure` panel, item `id`s; the key is in `cpKeys/{page key}`, teacher only, uploaded with `tools/checkpoint-keys/upload-keys.mjs` from a file kept outside the repo). `shared/js/el-checkpoint.js`: one question at a time, 45 s each, no going back, no copy or paste, leaving the page for more than 2 s sends it (⚡), one try (`students/{uid}/checkpoints/{key}`, create only in `firestore.rules`), corrections only when the teacher presses “📣 Release feedback” in the group tab's “🛡️ Recall checkpoints” box, which also has “✓ Accept”, “↺ Let them do it again” and ⚠️ when a checkpoint is 35 points or more above the student's usual marks (exam marks so far and recall warm-ups, `cpGap`). - **LS mark (Roger, 24 Oct 2026):** final exam 35 % · final product 25 % · grammar and vocabulary activities 20 % · other competences 20 %, recorded from every activity. Key competences are the communicative skills (CE2 Listening, CE3 Speaking, CE4 Reading, CE5 Writing): assessed in the exam and the final product. The other competences (CE1, CE6–CE10, transversal) form the 20 % block. **Grammar and vocabulary block (Roger, 26 Oct 2026):** inside the grammar and vocabulary block, recall checkpoints 50 % (first try of all four) · recall warm-ups 25 % (first try) · practice, grammar and mixed practice done by the session deadline 25 %. Scores alone never set the degree of a criterion whose indicators describe a task. With two pieces of evidence the later one weighs more; the dashboard suggests, the teacher confirms.
- **Scale:** NA 2.5 · AS 5 · AN 7.5 · AE 10 · no evidence 0. Mark to level: under 5 NA · 5–6.9 AS · 7–8.9 AN · 9–10 AE.

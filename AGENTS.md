## Route language and scaffolding
- **Beginners (Roger, 26 Oct 2026):** a fourth route, A1, below Insecure, for students starting almost from zero. Everything Insecure gets in Catalan, plus heavier scaffolding: a short core word list per LS practised with spaced retrieval (flashcards and recall every session), readings of 60–100 words built from that list at 95–98 % known words with Catalan hover glosses, multiple choice or matching instead of open answers, the slow audio copy with English subtitles from the first viewing, sentence frames with gaps and a word bank, the same final product with fewer requirements, feedback as a Catalan hint and a second try, and a short "how did I do it?" box. Same listenings and videos as everyone, mixed pairs in class (not Beginners with Beginners). Marked on the same NA–AE scale against CEFR A1 can-do statements. **Only the teacher sets it**, like every route (students can ask for it from their board; the teacher accepts or declines). **In code:** `elCa(level)` (`el-catalog.js`) says whether a route reads Catalan; LS2 engines order routes `beginners < insecure < safe < daring`, an untagged item is for every route, and a text, list or rubric with no `beginners` version falls back to `insecure` (`byLevel`, `forLevel`, `final-rubric.js`, the marking PDF, the study PDF). Every LS2 Learning Path page has a Beginners route button (students only see their own). LS1 pages have no Beginners version: `applyLevel` shows them the Insecure one. New LS content adds `beginners` keys where the A1 version differs (readings, questions, tasks, rubric), designed in the LS project. **Seasons & Celebrations (4 ESO) and Free Time, Live! (2 ESO) have Beginners content on every page since 26 Oct 2026** (docs/4eso/ls2-seasons-and-celebrations/overview.md and docs/2eso/ls2-free-time-live/overview.md, section "Beginners route"): the convention is that untagged items are for every route and `r: 'insecure'` marks what starts at Insecure. **The programmes** (docs/4eso/programme.md and docs/2eso/programme.md) carry the Beginners route since 26 Oct 2026 (Roger approved): a Beginners product bullet, core word list and A1 grammar block per LS, and a final chapter "Resum de gramàtica i lèxic · ruta Beginners (A1)" that is the reference for designing Beginners content in any LS. **Starter really differs from Base (Roger, 31 Oct 2026, thread "Explain Starter vs Base learning paths"):** Starter must not be just Base minus the hardest questions. (1) In LS2 engine pages whose `LP` has `starter: true` (2 ESO LP2–LP6, 4 ESO LP3–LP6; never add it to a path students have started), Starter picks typed answers (recall, grammar, mixed, vocabulary practice) from three buttons: the answer plus two distractors (`d: [...]` on the item if given, else forms of the bracketed verb, else a set of confusable words, else other answers of the step); a wrong pick is crossed out and they try again with the Catalan hint. Starter also starts listenings and videos on the `-slow` copy (videos with English subtitles on) and gets 16 s per question in Talk. From LS3 on, every LP sets `starter: true`. (2) An item with `only: ['insecure']` is shown only to the routes listed (`forLevel` in the engines, `el-items.js`, `lp-worksheet.js`): use it for Base practice of a basic form that Core and Plus already know (the grammar-that-comes-back rule). (3) Every vocabulary panel gives Starter its own short `core.beginners` list from the programme's Starter lexicon, and no step should be identical for Starter and Base: Starter gets an easier task or support, Base at least one or two items or requirements more.
- **Native (Roger, 30 Oct 2026):** a fifth route above Daring for a native speaker preparing Cambridge C2 Proficiency (4 ESO only for now); students see it as **Pro**. English throughout. **Only the teacher sets it** (`ask:false` in `EL_LEVELS`: students cannot ask for it). Same LS topic and Learning Paths, but C2 Proficiency-style tasks: Reading and Use of English (Part 5 texts, open cloze, word formation, key word transformations, multiple-choice cloze), listening with attitude and implication questions on the class audio, writing to Part 1/Part 2 tasks (article, essay, proposal, letter; 240–320 words; AI feedback on the four Cambridge writing subscales). **Speaking: no recording** (Roger): each speaking step is a `plan` panel (`ls2-engine.js` `R.plan`): the question, a strategy, the student's written plan (first ideas, the best ones and why, the order), then a model plan; handed in as `state.steps[id]`. **In code:** `data/native/<page>.js` sets `window.LP.native = { panelId: panel }`, loaded after the page's data file; `usePanels()` swaps those panels in by id (same ids, so progress, checkpoints, locks and the dashboard work). Getting started (own renderer) reads `window.GS_NATIVE` from `data/native/getting-started.js` (recall, vocab, practice, reading, listening, speaking plan). Anything without a Native version (other panels, LS1, 2 ESO, texts and lists in `byLevel`/`forLevel`) falls back to Daring, including the model page and rubric fallback. **Extra Pro panels:** a `LP.native` entry whose id is not in the page and has `after: '<panel id>'` is inserted after that panel (e.g. the real-video listening: one real native-speed YouTube video per Learning Path on its topic, `realvideo`, with C2 attitude/inference questions written from the video's captions; Roger 30 Oct 2026: real videos only, no generated audio); it is not in `EL_ACTIONS`, so it never changes progress. Getting started shows `GS_NATIVE.video` under the listening. The final product rubric has its own `native` entry (video and email rows on the Cambridge scales, mapped to NA–AE). `el-route-names.js` relabels Native → Pro except in “Native American(s)”; never start a student-facing sentence with a capitalised “Native” otherwise. ## Grammar
- Grammar content is specific to each Learning Situation. Do not assume the same grammar across future Learning Situations.

- Differentiate routes mainly through scaffolding, complexity, amount of support and learner independence.
- **Grammar that comes back (Roger, 30 Oct 2026, after 4 ESO LS2 LP2 repeated the LS1 Present Simple drills for every route):** when an LS recovers a grammar point an earlier LS already taught (an R in the programme's grammar table), the basic drills of the old form go only to Starter and Base (Beginners, Insecure: items with no `r:` or `r:'insecure'`). ## Flashcards
- Flashcard practice uses a **random set of up to 10 cards** (all cards if the deck has fewer). Do not make students work through the whole deck.
- The student **flips** the card, then chooses **Got it** or **Again**. The rating buttons stay disabled until the card is flipped. An **Again** card comes back before the student moves on, and the step is complete when every card in the set is **Got it**.
- "New set" gives a different random set. The counter keeps the format `Card 6 of 10 · Practising · 5/10 known`; Insecure shows it and the instructions in Catalan.
- Reuse the existing implementations (`flashcard-set` logic in the vocabulary pages and `shared/js/flashcard-set.js`) instead of writing a new one.
- **A new card appears front-up at once (problem report, 2ESO A, 5 Nov 2026):** when Got it / Again / Next / Previous changes the card, `flashcardSet.render()` turns it back with no animation (only on `always: true` pages, LS2; LS1 unchanged), so the next card's answer never shows while the card turns. Turning a card by hand still animates.

## Answer options
- **Randomize the answer options, never the questions.** Multiple-choice options and dropdown options are shuffled every time a question is shown, so the correct answer is not always in the same position. Question order stays as written.
- Use `learningSupport.shuffled()` / `learningSupport.shuffledIndexes()` from `shared/js/learning-path-support.js` (include that file in every page that renders questions). Keep each option's original value so scoring and feedback do not change.
- Do not change the wording of questions or options when shuffling.
